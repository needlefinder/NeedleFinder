## Audit findings

I found several **material contradictions**, as well as claims whose wording blurs different cohorts, thresholds, or metrics. I treated the dated JSON artifacts as the source of truth and compared the manuscript and six current tables against them. The most serious issues affect the 3T prostate result, CT pairing counts, interobserver testing, and statements that this benchmark does not support directly.

### Hard-to-verify or confusing claims

These are not all proven numerically false, but a skeptical reviewer could not reproduce or interpret them from the current wording.

- **“In-plane shaft error”** is used for multiple summaries: mean distance over matched slices, per-catheter means, Grubbs’ robust SD, and pairwise observer discrepancy. Define each estimator and its aggregation level. Include whether the mean is across catheters, slices, or patients.
- **“Tip error”** can mean 3D Euclidean endpoint distance, axial displacement, Winsorized axial SD, or dose-weighted 3D error. Label every reported tip metric with its dimension and weighting. The abstract’s “98.8% of squared tip variance longitudinal” also needs the denominator and decomposition equation.
- **“Severe adhesion”** is a patient-level inclusion rule based on catheter-neighbor distances “on shared axial slices,” but it is unclear whether the qualifying number counts catheters, pairs, or any slice-level encounter. State the exact algorithm and whether distances are 2D in-plane or 3D.
- **“Dual observer,” “consensus,” and “union”** are easy to conflate. Report `CY+HT` and `LB/AMe` as annotation sets, then define the 465 consensus pairs, 13 HT matches, and 35 extra traces; explain precisely how the 759-member union deduplicates tracks.
- **The human floor / non-inferiority language** risks suggesting the algorithm is no worse than humans on every error metric. The tests are endpoint-specific and use margins; report those results narrowly and state that the data do not establish equivalence for every outcome.
- **Dosimetry protocols** need a clearer operational description. “Transfer fixed plan” and “re-optimize on predicted catheters, deliver on reference geometry” are counterfactual simulations. State which catheters were matched, how unmatched/missed tracks were handled, what target/OAR envelopes were used, and whether dose values are absolute or percentage changes. The manuscript acknowledges standardized envelopes, but that limitation should accompany the prominent clinical dose claims.
- **Review simulation** assumes 8 seconds per reviewed tip and selects the most uncertain tips. Call it a simulated workflow wherever summarized; do not imply prospective clinician time or demonstrated clinical dose preservation.
- **“100% precision,” “zero false alarms,” and “perfect”** should always include the exact scan, model, crop, threshold, and denominator. For a single 14-catheter case, this is a descriptive result, not a stable estimate of generalization.
- **4D “tissue push” and “non-rigid displacement”** are algorithm-derived between-scan changes, not direct biomechanical measurements. Define registration, catheter persistence matching, distance-to-new-needle calculation, and how insertion timing is assigned before calling these direct in-vivo measurements.
- **“Zero-shot”** is used for models with differing SSL exposure, fine-tuning, and preprocessing. Define it as no labeled training on the target domain and disclose any unlabeled target-domain exposure separately.
- **“100% reference-free” and “zero leakage”** are categorical. Give the specific evaluation checks that establish these claims (what files/labels are excluded and how verified), especially because auxiliary annotations and external scans are used for pretraining.
- **Literature Table 5** is not verifiable from the supplied 2026-10-23 JSON artifacts; its publication-derived values need citation-level checking against the cited papers. Also the table’s `Pts` column mixes patient counts, treatments, and scan counts, while `Cath.` mixes catheters, fractions, plans, and volumes. Rename/qualify the units row by row.

I did not edit files. I did not assess the binary figures themselves; the figure-caption claims were checked against the manuscript/table/JSON text available here.
