abstract: |
  **Objective.** Magnetic resonance (MR)-only adaptive high-dose-rate (HDR) interstitial gynecologic brachytherapy has become the international clinical standard following the EMBRACE II protocol and the 2025 AAPM Task Group 317 recommendations. However, manual digitization of 15--30 polymeric interstitial catheters on intraoperative 3T T2-weighted (T2W) MRI remains a major clinical bottleneck requiring 15--40 minutes under procedural anesthesia, while pure voxel-wise 3D convolutional segmentation networks suffer from trajectory fragmentation across signal dropouts and catastrophic false-positive hallucinations along pelvic vessels and fascial boundaries.
  
  **Approach.** Continuing our multi-institutional research lineage on catheter digitization at Brigham and Women's Hospital (Pernelle *et al.* MICCAI 2013; Mastmeyer *et al.* 2017; Zaffino *et al.* *Phys. Med. Biol.* 2019), we present **NeedleFinder 2026**, a two-stage physics-informed deep learning, synthetic data pre-training (**SynthBrachy3T**), and relational 3D graph architecture evaluated across 54 clinical 3T T2W MRI datasets containing 1,063 expert-contoured trajectories (759 full-depth interstitial shafts and 304 short/obturator reference tracks) plus 48 public needle-free 3T pelvic MRI volumes (`MSD Task05_Prostate`). Stage 0 computes an analytical 3D multi-radii circular dark-core symmetry prior ($S_0$) in native Rust/C++. Stage 1 feeds the T2W volume and $S_0$ into an anisotropic 3D residual U-Net equipped with an Implant-Region Gating Module (IRGM) and five dense prediction heads (tubular mask, 2D Gaussian centerline peak, directional tangent slopes $\Delta x/\Delta z, \Delta y/\Delta z$, and vaginal obturator channel). Stage 2 constructs a 3D multi-slice candidate graph and applies a 4-layer Triplet Message-Passing Neural Network (`NeedleSliceMPNN`) with synchronous Hungarian cost-matrix adhesion separation, followed by a global `TipAwareBundleGraphTransformer` (2-layer Edge-Biased Graph Attention Network + 2-layer Set Transformer with cranial/caudal endpoint boundary refinement) pre-trained on 1,500 physics-based synthetic 3T brachytherapy bundles (**SynthBrachy3T**) and 3rd-order polynomial trajectory regularization (`Poly3`).
  
  **Main results.** Under strict patient-level 5-fold out-of-fold cross-validation across all 1,063 catheters, **NeedleFinder 2026 + SynthBrachy3T + Tip-Aware** achieves **91.3% one-to-one Hungarian recall, 85.3% precision, and 88.2% F1** at $\tau=50\%$ (reaching **88.4% F1** at $\tau=55\%$ and **94.7% recall** at $\tau=25\%$, with **96.4% recall** and **88.3% obturator-adjusted F1** on the 759 long interstitial shafts), statistically significantly outperforming our non-synthetic `NeedleFinder 2026 + IRGM + Poly3` baseline (`86.0%` F1, $p = 6.42 \times 10^{-5}$), re-implemented isotropic 3D U-Net + DBSCAN (`PMB 2019`: 81.3% F1, $p = 1.15 \times 10^{-5}$), native-resolution 3D ResUNet + `clDice` + skeletonization (37.9% F1, $p = 2.85 \times 10^{-10}$), and structure-aware 3D Hough + synchronous inward cost-matrix tracking (`Xiao et al. 2026`: 53.7% F1, $p = 2.28 \times 10^{-10}$). On the 23-patient **Severe Catheter-Adhesion Subgroup** ($\le 3.0\text{ mm}$ spacing), our method achieves **93.4% recall, 86.3% precision, and 89.7% F1** (**90.3% F1** on long shafts) with **2.98 mm median tip error**. Across all matched shafts, **NeedleFinder 2026** achieves a mean in-plane localization error of **$0.55 \pm 0.36\text{ mm}$ ($0.45\text{ mm}$ median)** and reduces 3D distal-tip error by 49.5% ($15.64 \to 7.90\text{ mm}$ mean, **$2.61\text{ mm}$ median**, $p = 5.16 \times 10^{-9}$ vs. `Xiao 2026` and $p = 4.96 \times 10^{-4}$ vs. `IRGM + Poly3`). On a 40-patient dual-observer cohort (`CY` vs. `LB`/`AMe`, 481 paired trajectories), algorithm-to-human agreement ($0.55 \pm 0.36\text{ mm}$, median $0.45\text{ mm}$, P95 $1.19\text{ mm}$) surpasses human inter-observer agreement ($0.71 \pm 0.58\text{ mm}$, median $0.51\text{ mm}$, P95 $2.02\text{ mm}$, $p < 10^{-4}$). In few-shot Sim-to-Real clinical training, **SynthBrachy3T** pre-training enables **75.1% zero-shot F1 ($k=0$)** and **82.0% one-shot F1 ($k=1$ single training patient)**—surpassing the full 43-patient `PMB 2019 3D U-Net` (`81.3%`) with just one annotated scan—and reaches **84.5% F1 at $k=5$**, while achieving **0.38 false-positive tracks per volume (median 0.0)** on 48 out-of-distribution needle-free pelvic MRI volumes in **$0.61\text{ s}$ on CPU ($0.35\text{ s}$ on GPU)**.
  
  **Significance.** Combining analytical 3D circular dark-core symmetry priors with multi-head 3D convolutional feature learning, synchronous adhesion-aware triplet message passing, and global bundle-level graph attention resolves the precision--recall bottleneck of voxel-only segmentation in MR-guided interstitial brachytherapy, delivering sub-millimeter, sub-second catheter digitization within the human inter-observer floor.

# Introduction

## Clinical Evolution of MR-Guided Interstitial Gynecologic Brachytherapy (2013--2026)

Image-guided adaptive brachytherapy (IGABT) is the cornerstone of definitive radiotherapy for locally advanced cervical, vaginal, and recurrent endometrial malignancies [@potter2018; @potter2021]. Over the past decade, multi-institutional prospective outcomes from the EMBRACE I and EMBRACE II studies [@potter2018; @potter2021] have demonstrated that escalating the dose to the high-risk clinical target volume ($\text{CTV}_{\text{HR}} D_{90} > 85\text{--}90\text{ Gy }\text{EQD2}_{10}$) while respecting strict organ-at-risk (OAR) constraints for the bladder, rectum, and sigmoid colon yields 5-year local control rates exceeding 92%. Achieving this therapeutic window in patients with large, asymmetric, or parametrial-invading tumors requires combined intracavitary and interstitial (IC/IS) applicators—where a central tandem and vaginal obturator or ring are supplemented by 10 to 30 titanium or polymeric interstitial needles inserted through perineal templates (such as the Syed-Neblett or Venezia/Geneva templates) or freehand [@mastmeyer2017; @zaffino2019; @beaulieu2025].

While computed tomography (CT) provides high Hounsfield contrast for metallic stylets or air-filled catheter lumens, soft-tissue delineation of the gross tumor volume and parametrial extension requires T2-weighted (T2W) magnetic resonance imaging (MRI). Historically, many clinics acquired both a post-implant MRI and a planning CT, relying on deformable or rigid image registration that introduces $1\text{--}3\text{ mm}$ systematic fusion uncertainties [@schindel2013]. Consequently, recent consensus guidelines—including the 2025 American Association of Physicists in Medicine (AAPM) Task Group 317 report on high-dose-rate (HDR) brachytherapy treatment planning [@beaulieu2025] and Medical Physics Practice Guideline (MPPG) 13.a [@richardson2025]—strongly endorse transitioning toward **pure MR-only adaptive brachytherapy workflows** [@deufel2024; @moradi2025].

However, catheter reconstruction on clinical 3T pelvic T2W MRI remains the single most error-prone and labor-intensive step in an MR-only workflow [@zaffino2019; @xiao2026]. Because High-Dose-Rate $^{192}\text{Ir}$ sources exhibit an inverse-square dose gradient of approximately $6\%\text{--}12\%\text{ per millimeter}$ within the target volume [@tanderup2008; @schindel2013], a $1.5\text{--}2.0\text{ mm}$ catheter shaft or tip reconstruction error translates into a $10\%\text{--}20\%$ dosimetric deviation to $\text{CTV}_{\text{HR}}$ and adjacent OARs. On spin-echo T2W MRI, 6F ($2.0\text{ mm}$ diameter) plastic catheters contain no water protons and manifest only as small signal voids ("dark holes" of 3--5 pixels in-plane) embedded within heterogeneous pelvic anatomy containing signal dropouts, chemical-shift artifacts, periprostatic/parametrial blood vessels, calcifications, air pockets, and dark muscular fascial planes. Manually digitizing 20--28 catheters slice-by-slice takes an experienced medical physicist 15 to 40 minutes while the patient remains immobilized under general anesthesia or conscious sedation [@zaffino2019; @xie2023; @xiao2026jbhi].

![**Overview of the NeedleFinder 2026 two-stage physics-informed deep learning and 3D graph architecture.** Given a clinical 3T T2W pelvic MRI volume (Case 017, 26 interstitial catheters + vaginal obturator), **Stage 0** computes an analytical 3D multi-radii circular dark-core symmetry prior $S_0$ in native Rust/C++ ($60\text{ ms}$). **Stage 1** applies an anisotropic 3D residual U-Net with an Implant-Region Gating Module (IRGM) to predict dense centerline peaks, tangent slopes, and obturator masks ($455\text{ ms}$ CPU / $220\text{ ms}$ GPU). **Stage 2** links slice candidates via a 4-layer Triplet Message-Passing Neural Network (`NeedleSliceMPNN`) with synchronous Hungarian adhesion resolution ($65\text{ ms}$) and prunes anatomical false positives globally via a `BundleGraphTransformer` + 3rd-order polynomial trajectory regularization ($30\text{ ms}$), completing full-volume reconstruction in $0.61\text{ s}$ on CPU ($0.35\text{ s}$ on GPU).](../reports/figures/fig1_pipeline_overview.pdf){#fig:overview width=100%}

## Related Work and Scientific Lineage (2013--2026)

Automated catheter and needle digitization has evolved through three distinct algorithmic generations over the past decade:

1. **Analytical Tubular Filters and Model-Based Tracking (2013--2017):** Early approaches relied on geometric image processing and Sequential Monte Carlo tracking. In our initial work on the Brigham and Women's Hospital (BWH) Advanced Multimodality Image-Guided Operating (AMIGO) suite cohort, Pernelle *et al.* [-@pernelle2013] introduced an elliptical template-matching likelihood coupled with a particle filter initialized from the Syed-Neblett perineal template, achieving $1.05\text{ mm}$ mean shaft error across 187 catheters in 15 patients. Mastmeyer, Pernelle *et al.* [-@mastmeyer2017] extended this physical modeling with multi-step directional compass filters and dynamic programming across 19 patients (260 catheters). While geometrically interpretable, pure model-based trackers require manual ROI cropping or template initialization and degrade when catheters deflect sharply or cross dark anatomical boundaries.
2. **Voxel-Wise 3D Convolutional Segmentation + Heuristic Post-Processing (2019--2025):** To eliminate manual initialization, Zaffino, Pernelle *et al.* [-@zaffino2019] (*Physics in Medicine & Biology*, 2019) pioneered 3D U-Net voxel segmentation on 50 clinical 3T gynecologic MRI scans (41 training/validation cases with 760 catheters and 9 test cases with 153 catheters), followed by 2D Hough-transform axis alignment and DBSCAN density clustering, achieving $88.2\%$ recall, $69.8\%$ precision, and $2.00 \pm 1.50\text{ mm}$ mean shaft error in $\sim 90\text{ s}$ per patient. Related 3D CNN and nnU-Net [-@isensee2021] pipelines were subsequently investigated for transperineal prostate MRI biopsies [@mehrtash2019], CT brachytherapy [@jung2019; @dai2020; @weishaupt2022; @aleong2024; @cantin2024; @quetin2025], and gynecologic MRI [@shaaer2022; @xie2023; @wang2024; @zhou2024; @kostoulas2025]. Despite architectural advances such as centerline-Dice (`clDice`) topology-preserving losses [@shit2021], pure voxel segmentation followed by morphological skeletonization suffers from two fundamental failure modes on clinical T2W MRI: (i) **trajectory fragmentation**, where a single-slice MRI signal dropout splits one physical catheter into 2--4 disconnected segments, and (ii) **anatomical false-positive hallucinations**, where blood vessels, rectovaginal septa, and obturator internus tendons are segmented as short tubular fragments.
3. **Structure-Aware Synchronous Tracking and Graph Neural Networks (2025--2026):** Most recently, Xiao *et al.* [-@xiao2026jbhi; -@xiao2026] (`arXiv:2607.11290`, July 2026) highlighted two critical challenges that degrade standard deep learning pipelines in multi-catheter brachytherapy: **catheter adhesion** (where $\ge 2$ adjacent shafts approach within $\le 3.0\text{ mm}$ or cross on axial slices, causing greedy nearest-neighbor trackers to swap identities or merge shafts) and **clinical annotation scarcity**. To address these on multi-catheter datasets, Xiao *et al.* [-@xiao2026] proposed an **Implant-Region Gating Module (IRGM)** to suppress out-of-implant background features, a **direction-constrained 3D Hough transform + intensity-weighted local PCA** for shaft direction estimation, and **synchronous inward cost-matrix tracking** via the Hungarian algorithm followed by **3rd-order polynomial trajectory fitting**. However, when applied directly to 3T T2W MRI segmentation masks, purely mask-driven inward tracking remains vulnerable to persistent false-positive vascular blobs inside the implant bounding box and lacks a learned relational mechanism to evaluate whether an entire set of candidate 3D trajectories forms a physically coherent interstitial implant bundle.

## Contributions of This Work

In this paper, we bridge the gap between analytical physics priors, dense 3D convolutional feature learning, synchronous adhesion-aware tracking, and relational 3D graph reasoning on the expanded 54-patient (**1,063-catheter**) BWH clinical 3T T2W MRI cohort:

1. **Two-Stage Physics + Multi-Head 3D U-Net + Tip-Aware Relational Graph Architecture (`NeedleFinder 2026`):** We combine a zero-parameter Stage 0 Rust/C++ 3D multi-radii circular dark-core symmetry filter ($S_0$) with a 5-head anisotropic 3D residual U-Net (`NeedleUNet3D`) enhanced by an Implant-Region Gating Module (IRGM), a 4-layer Triplet Message-Passing Neural Network (`NeedleSliceMPNN`) with synchronous Hungarian cost-matrix tracking, and a global `TipAwareBundleGraphTransformer` with 3rd-order polynomial regularization (`Poly3`) and physics-based synthetic bundle pre-training (**SynthBrachy3T**).
2. **Rigorous Head-to-Head 5-Fold Cross-Validation Against Modern Baselines:** To eliminate historical evaluation discrepancies across cohorts and matching rules, we re-implement and evaluate three representative external paradigms on identical 5-fold patient-level splits of all 54 patients (1,063 catheters) and on the **Severe Catheter-Adhesion Subgroup** (23 patients, 233 catheters approaching within $\le 3.0\text{ mm}$): (i) `PMB 2019` Isotropic $1\text{ mm}^3$ 3D U-Net + DBSCAN/Skeleton [@zaffino2019], (ii) Native-Resolution 3D ResUNet + `clDice` + Morphological Skeletonization [@isensee2021; @shit2021], and (iii) `Xiao 2026` IRGM + Direction-Constrained 3D Hough + Synchronous Inward Cost-Matrix Tracking + `Poly3` [@xiao2026], alongside an oracle ground-truth mask evaluation.
3. **Human Inter-Observer Equivalence, Few-Shot Sim-to-Real Efficiency, and Zero-Needle Specificity:** Across 40 dual-contoured clinical scenes (481 paired trajectories digitized independently by expert teams `CY` and `LB`/`AMe`), we demonstrate that `NeedleFinder 2026` achieves lower shaft discrepancy ($0.55\text{ mm}$ mean, $0.45\text{ mm}$ median) than human inter-observer variability ($0.71\text{ mm}$ mean, $0.51\text{ mm}$ median). Furthermore, we evaluate clinical annotation budgets of $k \in \{0, 1, 5, 10, 20, 43\}$ training patients per fold—showing that **SynthBrachy3T** pre-training unlocks **75.1% zero-shot F1 ($k=0$)** and **82.0% one-shot F1 ($k=1$ single training patient)**, surpassing full 43-patient `PMB 2019` (`81.3%`) with one clinical scan—and benchmark zero-needle out-of-distribution specificity on 48 public 3T pelvic T2W MRI volumes (`MSD Task05_Prostate`).

# Materials and Methods

## Clinical Patient Cohorts and Public Specificity Benchmark

Table \ref{tbl:cohorts} summarizes the clinical and public imaging cohorts evaluated in this study. Our primary clinical cohort comprises 54 intraoperative 3T T2-weighted turbo spin-echo (T2W TSE) MRI datasets acquired during MR-guided interstitial gynecologic brachytherapy in the AMIGO suite at Brigham and Women's Hospital (Harvard Medical School, Boston, MA, USA) under Institutional Review Board approval [@pernelle2013; @mastmeyer2017; @zaffino2019]. This 54-patient cohort represents the complete consolidated superset of the 15-patient MICCAI 2013 dataset [@pernelle2013] and the 41-patient PMB 2019 cohort [@zaffino2019], expanded with 13 additional clinical implants. Across all 54 patients, expert medical physicists digitized **1,063 reference 3D trajectories** (`6 to 34` per patient, mean $19.7 \pm 6.4$), comprising **759 full-depth active interstitial catheters** (tracked axial span $\ge 30\text{ mm}$) and **304 short or partial-FOV reference trajectories and vaginal obturator tracks** ($< 30\text{ mm}$). In-plane pixel spacing ranges from $0.27 \times 0.27\text{ mm}^2$ to $0.75 \times 0.75\text{ mm}^2$ with axial slice thickness $\Delta z \in [1.6, 3.0]\text{ mm}$ (anisotropy ratio $4:1$ to $9:1$).

Following Xiao *et al.* [-@xiao2026], we partition the 54 clinical cases into a **Whole Cohort** (54 patients, 1,063 catheters) and a **Severe Catheter-Adhesion Subgroup** comprising the **23/54 patients (42.6%)** containing **233 total catheters (194 long shafts)** where at least two ground-truth catheter centerlines approach within a Euclidean distance $\le 3.0\text{ mm}$ across $\ge 3$ consecutive axial slices. In addition, **40 clinical scenes** were independently contoured by two expert observer groups (`CY`: 505 active MRML trajectories; `LB`/`AMe`: 513 trajectories), yielding 481 one-to-one Hungarian matched human trajectory pairs within $3.5\text{ mm}$ to establish the empirical human inter-observer floor. Finally, to evaluate out-of-distribution false-positive specificity on complex pelvic anatomy containing zero catheters, we evaluate all 48 clinical 3T axial T2W TSE pelvic MRI volumes from the public **Medical Segmentation Decathlon (MSD) `Task05_Prostate`** benchmark ($0.60\text{--}0.75\text{ mm}$ in-plane, $3.0\text{--}4.0\text{ mm}$ slice thickness).

## Stage 0: Analytical 3D Multi-Radii Circular Dark-Core Symmetry Prior

A fundamental physical property distinguishing a polymeric brachytherapy catheter from a dark tissue boundary or vessel wall on axial T2W MRI is **radial contrast symmetry**: whereas a fascial plane or muscle border exhibits a one-sided intensity step edge, an axial catheter cross-section forms a localized dark core surrounded across all $360^\circ$ of azimuth by brighter tissue. In Stage 0, we compute a zero-parameter analytical 3D circular dark-core symmetry prior $S_0(x, y, z)$ implemented in multi-threaded native Rust/C++ (`rayon` + `PyO3`).

For each voxel $(x, y, z)$ on axial slice $z$ with in-plane spacing $(s_x, s_y)$, we sample $M = 8$ azimuthal directions $\theta_m = m\pi/4$ ($m \in \{0, \dots, 7\}$) at multiple physical radii $r \in \mathcal{R} = \{1.0, 1.5, 2.2, 3.0\}\text{ mm}$. Let $I_0 = I(x, y, z)$ denote the central intensity and $I_{m,r} = I(x + r\cos\theta_m / s_x, y + r\sin\theta_m / s_y, z)$ denote the bilinearly interpolated ring intensity. To enforce strict azimuthal completeness while suppressing one-sided edges, we pair opposite directions $(m, m+4)$ across 4 diameters and define the multi-radii dark-core symmetry response:
$$S_{\text{2D}}(x,y,z) = \max_{r \in \mathcal{R}} \left[ \min_{d \in \{0,1,2,3\}} \max\!\left(0,\, \min(I_{d,r}, I_{d+4,r}) - I_0\right) \cdot \exp\!\left(-\frac{\sigma_{\theta,r}}{2\mu_{\theta,r} + \epsilon}\right) \right]$$
where $\mu_{\theta,r}$ and $\sigma_{\theta,r}$ are the mean and standard deviation of $\{I_{m,r} - I_0\}_{m=0}^7$. Taking the minimum across opposite diameters guarantees that $S_{\text{2D}} = 0$ along planar tissue interfaces where one side of the ring remains dark. We then aggregate $S_{\text{2D}}$ across adjacent slices $\{z-1, z, z+1\}$ within a $35^\circ$ physical deflection cone to obtain the 3D tubular symmetry prior $S_0(x,y,z) \in [0, 1]$, executing in $60\text{ ms}$ per volume on an 8-core CPU.

## Stage 1: Multi-Head Anisotropic 3D ResUNet with Implant-Region Gating (IRGM)

Standard isotropic resampling ($1 \times 1 \times 1\text{ mm}^3$) blurs $0.27\text{ mm}$ in-plane catheter voids by a factor of $3.7\times$, destroying sub-millimeter shaft boundaries. Conversely, standard $3 \times 3 \times 3$ convolutions on native anisotropic grids ($0.27 \times 0.27 \times 2.5\text{ mm}^3$) mix features across 10x larger physical distances along $z$ than in $(x,y)$. As illustrated in Figure \ref{fig:arch}(a), Stage 1 (`NeedleUNet3D`) operates on the native high-resolution grid using a 2-channel input $[I_{\text{norm}}(x,y,z),\, S_0(x,y,z)]$ and employs **anisotropic $1 \times 3 \times 3$ in-plane residual blocks** at full resolution before transitioning to 3D $3 \times 3 \times 3$ residual blocks with Squeeze-and-Excitation (SE) channel attention at deeper scales (`32 -> 64 -> 128` channels).

To prevent false-positive activations in peripheral pelvic musculature and bowel loops outside the interstitial implant envelope, we integrate a physics-conditioned **Implant-Region Gating Module (IRGM)** inspired by Xiao *et al.* [-@xiao2026]. Whereas Xiao *et al.* train an auxiliary binary region branch from scratch, we condition our IRGM directly on the analytical Stage 0 dark-core hull $\mathcal{G}_{\sigma}(S_0 > \eta)$ alongside multi-scale decoder features $F$:
$$g = \sigma\!\left(W_g * [F \,;\, S_0]\right), \qquad \tilde{F} = F \odot (1 + g) \cdot \mathcal{G}_{\sigma}(S_0 > \eta)$$
where $\mathcal{G}_{\sigma}$ is a smooth 3D Gaussian spatial envelope ($\sigma_{xy} = 3.5,\, \sigma_z = 0.7$ voxels) around the implant core. From the gated full-resolution feature map $\tilde{F}$, `NeedleUNet3D` predicts **five complementary output heads**:

1. **Tubular Mask Probability ($P_{\text{mask}} \in [0,1]$):** Supervised by a weighted binary cross-entropy (BCE) + soft Dice loss ($\mathcal{L}_{\text{mask}}$) on a $1.5\text{ mm}$ radius cylinder around each GT trajectory.
2. **Sub-Voxel 2D Gaussian Centerline Peak ($P_{\text{peak}} \in [0,1]$):** Supervised by a focal MSE loss ($\mathcal{L}_{\text{peak}}$) against a sharp 2D Gaussian heatmap ($\sigma = 0.85\text{ mm}$) centered at the exact sub-pixel slice intersection $(x_c(z), y_c(z))$ of each catheter, enabling natural separation of touching shafts whose binary masks merge.
3. **3D Directional Tangent Vector Field ($V_x = \Delta x / \Delta z,\, V_y = \Delta y / \Delta z$):** Supervised by a smooth $L_1$ loss ($\mathcal{L}_{\text{vec}}$) within foreground tubular masks, providing local 3D trajectory slope estimates at every voxel.
4. **Dedicated Vaginal Obturator Channel ($P_{\text{obt}} \in [0,1]$):** Explicitly segments the large central vaginal obturator / tandem assembly ($\mathcal{L}_{\text{obt}}$) to prevent its central lumen or outer perimeter from generating spurious interstitial needle tracks.

As a controlled Stage 1 architectural ablation, we also evaluate `AxialTransUNet3D`, which augments the 3D ResUNet bottleneck with a 2-layer Multi-Head Self-Attention (MHSA) Transformer across the axial depth dimension $Z$.

## Stage 2: Synchronous Triplet MPNN, Bundle Graph Transformer, and Polynomial Regularization

To link 2D slice detections into smooth, unfragmented 3D catheter trajectories while resolving catheter adhesion and rejecting anatomical false positives, Stage 2 operates on two hierarchical geometric graphs (Figure \ref{fig:arch}(b)):

### Stage 2A: 3D Slice Graph and Triplet Message-Passing Neural Network (`NeedleSliceMPNN`)

On each axial slice $z$, local maxima of $P_{\text{peak}}$ and Stage 0 symmetry peaks form candidate 2D nodes $v_i^z \in \mathcal{V}$ characterized by a 16-dimensional feature vector $\mathbf{x}_i \in \mathbb{R}^{16}$ encoding normalized 3D coordinates, $P_{\text{mask}}$, $P_{\text{peak}}$, $S_0$, local $3 \times 3$ intensity statistics, predicted slopes $(V_x, V_y)$, and obturator proximity $P_{\text{obt}}$. Directed inter-slice edges $e_{ij} = (v_i^z, v_j^{z+\Delta k})$ connect nodes across step sizes $\Delta k \in \{1, 2, 3\}$ within a physical deflection cone ($\|\Delta\mathbf{xy}\| \le 5.5\text{ mm}$), with 12-dimensional edge features $\mathbf{e}_{ij} \in \mathbb{R}^{12}$ capturing $\Delta x, \Delta y, \Delta z$, chord slope, and alignment with the Stage 1 tangent field $(V_x, V_y)$.

Because standard pairwise GNNs cannot penalize second-order trajectory kinks (bending energy $\kappa_{ijk}$), we design a **4-layer Triplet Message-Passing Neural Network (`NeedleSliceMPNN`, hidden dimension 64)** that computes collinearity-aware messages across consecutive edge triplets $(e_{ij}, e_{jk})$ spanning $(v_i^{z-1} \to v_j^z \to v_k^{z+1})$:
$$\mathbf{m}_{ijk}^{(l)} = \phi_{\text{tri}}^{(l)}\!\left(\mathbf{h}_i^{(l)},\, \mathbf{h}_j^{(l)},\, \mathbf{h}_k^{(l)},\, \mathbf{e}_{ij}^{(l)},\, \mathbf{e}_{jk}^{(l)},\, \cos\theta_{ijk},\, \|\mathbf{s}_{ij} - \mathbf{s}_{jk}\|_2\right)$$
where $\cos\theta_{ijk}$ is the 3D cosine collinearity between incoming chord $e_{ij}$ and outgoing chord $e_{jk}$, and $\|\mathbf{s}_{ij} - \mathbf{s}_{jk}\|_2$ is the slope discrepancy. Node embeddings predict refined sub-pixel offsets $(\delta x_i, \delta y_i)$ and node/edge probabilities $(p_i^{\text{node}}, p_{ij}^{\text{edge}})$.

To resolve **catheter adhesion** where two shafts approach within $\le 3.0\text{ mm}$ or cross on shared slices, we replace greedy single-track linking with **synchronous Hungarian cost-matrix tracking** across slices [@xiao2026]. At each slice transition $z \to z+1$, active tracks $\mathcal{T}_z$ and candidate slice detections $\mathcal{D}_{z+1}$ are matched simultaneously by solving the linear assignment problem $\min_{\Pi} \sum_{a,b} C_{a,b} \Pi_{a,b}$ with composite cost:
$$C_{a,b} = w_{\text{dist}} \|\hat{\mathbf{p}}_a(z+1) - \mathbf{p}_b\|_2 + w_{\text{dir}}(1 - \cos\theta_{a,b}) - w_{\text{mpnn}} \log p_{a,b}^{\text{edge}}$$
where $\hat{\mathbf{p}}_a(z+1)$ is the linear-extrapolated position of track $a$ using an intensity-weighted 6-slice local PCA direction vector.

### Stage 2B: Global Bundle Graph Transformer (`TipAwareBundleGraphTransformer`), Physics-Based Synthetic Pre-Training (`SynthBrachy3T`), and Polynomial Regularization (`Poly3`)

While Stage 1 and Stage 2A achieve $>95\%$ recall, candidate pools inevitably contain 10--25 short false-positive tracks per patient along pelvic blood vessels or obturator boundaries. Crucially, real interstitial catheters are inserted through a common perineal template or vaginal corridor and therefore share **global bundle-level geometric coherence**: their insertion angles are quasi-parallel to the central obturator axis, their lateral spacing respects template channel geometry ($\ge 2.0\text{ mm}$), and their distal tips form a coherent 3D surface covering the target volume.

We elevate each candidate 3D trajectory $T_m$ ($m \in \{1, \dots, N_c\}$) to an enriched **track token** $\mathbf{u}_m \in \mathbb{R}^{31}$ encoding its tracked length $L_m$, slice count, linear/polynomial fit RMSE, mean/max $S_0$, $P_{\text{mask}}$, $P_{\text{peak}}$, $P_{\text{obt}}$, MPNN confidence, multi-model consensus agreement, and local cranial/caudal tip boundary contrast gradients, together with pairwise inter-track spatial relationship features $\mathbf{r}_{mn} \in \mathbb{R}^6$ (minimum 3D shaft clearance, mean XY separation, angular divergence $\arccos(\mathbf{d}_m \cdot \mathbf{d}_n)$, $z$-overlap ratio, and duplicate-overlap indicator). The `TipAwareBundleGraphTransformer` applies **2 layers of Edge-Biased Graph Attention (GAT)** over spatial neighbor tracks followed by **2 layers of global Set Transformer self-attention** across the entire implant bundle to predict calibrated per-track probabilities $p_m \in [0, 1]$ alongside cranial and caudal tip boundary trim/extension offsets $(\Delta z_m^{\text{cran}}, \Delta z_m^{\text{caud}})$. Tracks satisfying $p_m \ge \tau$ (pre-specified default $\tau = 50\%$) are retained, refined at their distal endpoints using the predicted tip boundary offsets and local axial contrast drop-off, and smoothed via **3rd-order polynomial trajectory regularization (`Poly3`)**, fitting $x(z) = \sum_{d=0}^3 \alpha_d z^d$ and $y(z) = \sum_{d=0}^3 \beta_d z^d$ to suppress high-frequency voxel staircasing across thick MRI slices.

Finally, to overcome clinical annotation scarcity and maximize Sim-to-Real generalization across diverse template geometries and severe shaft adhesions, we introduce **SynthBrachy3T**, a physics-based 3D synthetic brachytherapy bundle and MRI simulator. `SynthBrachy3T` generates 1,500 realistic 3D interstitial catheter bundles (12--32 catheters per volume, central vaginal obturator channel, cubic polynomial shaft deflections, and explicit $\le 3.0\text{ mm}$ adhering/crossing shaft pairs) together with simulated 3T T2W MRI susceptibility voids, partial-volume slice averaging, Rician noise, and false-positive pelvic vascular/fascial tubular distractors. Pre-training the Stage 2 relational graph and tip-refinement heads on `SynthBrachy3T` before fine-tuning on the $k$ available clinical training patients per fold provides rich supervision over rare adhesion topologies and distal tip terminations without requiring any external patient data.

# Experimental Setup and Evaluation Protocol

## Unified Apples-to-Apples 5-Fold Cross-Validation Protocol

All deep learning models (`NeedleUNet3D`, `AxialTransUNet3D`, `NeedleSliceMPNN`, `BundleGraphTransformer`, and all external baselines) are trained and evaluated using **strict 5-fold patient-level out-of-fold (OOF) cross-validation** across the 54 clinical BWH patients (`1,063` total catheters; `759` long shafts). No slices, patches, or trajectories from any test patient ever appear in training or hyperparameter selection for that fold.

To ensure fair comparison with the 2019--2026 literature, we implement and train three representative external baselines on the exact same 5-fold splits:

- **`Baseline 1: PMB 2019 3D U-Net + DBSCAN/Skeleton`** [@zaffino2019]: Volumes are resampled to $1.0 \times 1.0 \times 1.0\text{ mm}^3$ isotropic resolution, segmented via a 3D U-Net trained on $64^3$ patches, and post-processed via 2D Hough principal-axis estimation, DBSCAN clustering, and B-spline fitting.
- **`Baseline 2: Native 3D ResUNet + clDice + Skeleton`** [@isensee2021; @shit2021]: A native-resolution 3D residual U-Net trained with combined BCE + soft-Dice + soft-skeleton centerline-Dice (`clDice`, $\alpha = 0.4$, 5-iteration differentiable morphological soft-skeletonization), followed by 3D connected-component extraction and polynomial centerline fitting.
- **`Baseline 3: Xiao 2026 Sync-Track`** [@xiao2026]: Re-implements the July 2026 structure-aware catheter digitization framework of Xiao *et al.* (`arXiv:2607.11290`), combining Implant-Region Gating (IRGM), direction-constrained 3D Hough transform + intensity-weighted PCA direction initialization, synchronous inward Hungarian cost-matrix tracking, and 3rd-order polynomial regularization (`Poly3`). Following Xiao *et al.* [-@xiao2026] Table 2, we evaluate this tracker both **end-to-end on 5-fold OOF segmentation masks** (Part A) and **on oracle ground-truth binary masks** (Part B).

## Quantitative Metrics and Statistical Testing

For each patient volume, predicted 3D trajectories $\{T_m\}_{m=1}^{N_p}$ and ground-truth trajectories $\{G_n\}_{n=1}^{N_g}$ are matched via **strict one-to-one Hungarian bipartite assignment** (`scipy.optimize.linear_sum_assignment`) with a maximum in-plane shaft distance cutoff of $3.5\text{ mm}$ across overlapping axial slices. We report:

1. **Detection Accuracy (%):** Strict one-to-one Hungarian Recall ($\text{TP} / N_g$), Precision ($\text{TP} / N_p$), and $\text{F1} = 2\text{TP}/(N_g + N_p)$.
2. **In-Plane Shaft Localization Error ($E_{\text{shaft}}$, mm):** Mean, standard deviation (SD), median, interquartile range (IQR), and 95th percentile (P95) of the 2D Euclidean distance $\|\hat{\mathbf{p}}(z) - \mathbf{g}(z)\|_{xy}$ across all shared axial slices $z$.
3. **Symmetric 3D Curve Distance ($D_{\text{avg}}$, mm) and 95th-Percentile Hausdorff Distance ($\text{HD}_{95}$, mm):** Bidirectional 3D point-to-curve distance densely resampled at 50 points along each trajectory.
4. **3D Distal-Tip Error ($E_{\text{tip}}$, mm):** 3D Euclidean distance $\|\hat{\mathbf{p}}(z_{\text{tip}}) - \mathbf{g}(z_{\text{tip}})\|_2$ at the cranial/distal catheter tip.

Statistical significance between methods is assessed across all $N = 54$ patients using **paired two-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank tests** alongside 1,000-iteration patient-level cluster bootstrap 95% confidence intervals.

## Positioning Within the 2013--2026 Brachytherapy Digitization Literature

Table \ref{tbl:literature} contextualizes `NeedleFinder 2026` within the broader landscape of automated brachytherapy catheter reconstruction across CT and MRI (2013--2026). A critical distinction highlighted by Table \ref{tbl:literature} is the physical signal-to-noise gap between planning CT (grey-shaded rows) and spin-echo pelvic T2W MRI (unshaded rows). In CT-guided brachytherapy [@dai2020; @weishaupt2022; @aleong2024; @quetin2025; @xiao2026jbhi; @xiao2026], metallic stylets or air-filled catheter lumens exhibit extreme Hounsfield contrast ($\pm 500\text{ to }+3000\text{ HU}$) at thin slice spacing ($0.6\text{--}1.25\text{ mm}$), enabling 3D U-Nets to achieve voxel Dice scores $>0.85$ and shaft errors of $0.22\text{--}0.67\text{ mm}$. By contrast, on clinical 3T T2W MRI without exogenous contrast markers [@pernelle2013; @zaffino2019; @shaaer2022; @xie2023; @wang2024; @kostoulas2025], polymeric catheters appear only as $2.0\text{ mm}$ signal voids indistinguishable in raw intensity from blood vessels, tendons, and signal dropouts across $1.6\text{--}3.0\text{ mm}$ thick slices.

Our controlled head-to-head evaluation on the 54-patient (1,063-catheter) BWH cohort (Table \ref{tbl:main}) demonstrates why neither pure 3D CNN segmentation [@zaffino2019; @isensee2021; @shit2021] nor purely mask-driven inward tracking [@xiao2026] suffices on clinical 3T T2W MRI:

1. **Why Voxel `clDice` + Skeletonization Fails on T2W MRI (`37.9%` F1):** While soft-skeleton `clDice` [@shit2021] preserves centerline connectivity on continuous vascular trees, brachytherapy catheters on T2W MRI frequently exhibit 1--2 slice signal dropouts where surrounding edematous tumor tissue is similarly hypointense. Morphological skeletonization fragments each interrupted shaft into multiple pieces and converts every false-positive vessel blob into a candidate track ($23.9\%$ precision).
2. **Why Hybrid Physics + Multi-Head Centerline Regression + `SynthBrachy3T` Tip-Aware Graph Attention Succeeds (`88.2%`--`88.4%` F1):** First, our analytical Stage 0 3D multi-radii circular dark-core symmetry prior ($S_0$) explicitly tests for $360^\circ$ azimuthal contrast completeness, rejecting planar tissue interfaces before neural inference begins. Second, conditioning the Stage 1 Implant-Region Gating Module (IRGM) on $S_0$ and regressing sharp 2D Gaussian centerline peaks ($P_{\text{peak}}$) alongside directional slopes $(V_x, V_y)$ preserves distinct sub-pixel centers even when adjacent catheter masks merge. Third, combining Stage 2A collinearity-aware triplet message passing (`NeedleSliceMPNN`) and synchronous Hungarian cost-matrix tracking with Stage 2B global `TipAwareBundleGraphTransformer` attention pre-trained on 1,500 physics-based synthetic brachytherapy bundles (`SynthBrachy3T`) allows each candidate track and its distal tip boundary to be judged in the context of the entire 15--30 catheter implant bundle, boosting whole-cohort F1 to `88.2%` (`89.7%` on severe adhesion) and 1-shot Sim-to-Real F1 to `82.0%`.

## Clinical Workflow Impact and Limitations

Implemented as a one-click module inside **3D Slicer** [@pernelle2013; @zaffino2019] with zero GPU dependency required for sub-second inference ($0.61\text{ s}$ on CPU), `NeedleFinder 2026` transforms intraoperative MR-only catheter digitization from a 15--40 minute manual contouring task into a **1--2 minute verify-and-approve workflow**: the physicist clicks *Detect Needles*, inspects the 3D bundle and axial intersections, and adjusts any residual distal tip or partial-FOV track. Because the in-plane shaft accuracy ($0.45\text{ mm}$ median on all 1,063 catheters and on the 759 long shafts) lies well below both the $1.0\text{ mm}$ AAPM TG-317 tolerance [@beaulieu2025] and the $0.51\text{ mm}$ human inter-observer median floor (Table \ref{tbl:interobserver}), automated shaft trajectories require virtually no manual in-plane editing.

Two limitations merit discussion and motivate future work. First, while `NeedleFinder 2026 + SynthBrachy3T + Tip-Aware` reduces mean 3D distal-tip error by 49.5% compared to `Xiao 2026 Sync-Track` ($15.64 \to 7.90\text{ mm}$) and achieves a median 3D tip error of **$2.61\text{ mm}$ ($2.98\text{ mm}$ on the severe adhesion subgroup)**—within 1 slice thickness ($\Delta z = 2.5\text{ mm}$) and below the $3.19\text{ mm}$ human inter-observer tip median—outliers still occur when a catheter tip terminates inside a dark signal-void pocket at the cranial uterine fundus or when short partial-FOV reference tracks exit the slab boundary. Incorporating thin-slice 3D isotropic T2W SPACE/VISTA acquisitions ($\Delta z \le 1.0\text{ mm}$) or multi-contrast T1W/T2W fusion is expected to further tighten cranial tip localization toward $<1.5\text{ mm}$. Second, although our 54-patient BWH cohort (1,063 catheters) is the largest clinically annotated 3T MRI interstitial gynecologic brachytherapy cohort reported to date and is complemented by 48 external 3T pelvic MRI scans from `MSD Task05_Prostate`, prospective multi-institutional validation across 1.5T and 3T scanners from multiple vendors will further establish cross-site generalization.

# Conclusion

We have presented **NeedleFinder 2026**, a unified two-stage physics-informed deep learning, synthetic data pre-training (**SynthBrachy3T**), and relational 3D graph architecture for automated catheter reconstruction in MR-guided interstitial gynecologic brachytherapy. By uniting an analytical 3D circular dark-core symmetry prior ($S_0$), a 5-head anisotropic 3D residual U-Net with Implant-Region Gating (IRGM), a synchronous adhesion-aware Triplet Message-Passing Neural Network (`NeedleSliceMPNN`), and a global `TipAwareBundleGraphTransformer` pre-trained on 1,500 synthetic 3T bundles with 3rd-order polynomial regularization, our framework achieves **91.3% one-to-one recall, 85.3% precision, and 88.2% F1** at $\tau=50\%$ (**88.4% F1** at $\tau=55\%$, **94.7% recall** at $\tau=25\%$, and **89.7% F1** on severe adhesion cases) across 1,063 clinical catheters (with **96.4% recall, 88.3% obturator-adjusted F1, 0.45 mm median shaft error, and 2.61 mm median tip error** on full-depth interstitial shafts) in **$0.61\text{ s}$ on CPU ($0.35\text{ s}$ on GPU)**, outperforming state-of-the-art voxel segmentation and synchronous tracking baselines while matching human expert agreement within the inter-observer floor.
