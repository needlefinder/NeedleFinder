\begin{tablenotes}[flushleft]\scriptsize
\item Rec./Prec./F1: strict one-to-one Hungarian matching ($\le 3.5$\,mm shaft threshold); \Eshaft: in-plane centerline distance on matched slices; \Etip: 3D distal-tip distance (P95 on whole cohort: $27.27$\,mm for \NF{} \texttt{4b} and $27.42$\,mm for \texttt{4d} vs.\ $43.23$\,mm for Aleong/Kim, $45.76$\,mm for clDice, $46.68$\,mm for Dai 2020, $50.23$\,mm for Kostoulas 2025, $52.70$\,mm for PMB 2019, and $62.69$\,mm for Xiao 2026).
\item[a] Peer-reviewed MRI baseline (Zaffino \emph{et al.}\ 2019, \emph{Phys.\ Med.\ Biol.}): $1\times1\times1$\,mm$^3$ isotropic 3D U-Net, DBSCAN clustering, and polynomial fitting.
\item[b] Peer-reviewed tubular topology baseline (Shit \emph{et al.}\ 2021, \emph{CVPR}): native-resolution 3D ResUNet trained with Dice + focal + soft-skeleton clDice loss, followed by ridge extraction and chaining.
\item[c] Peer-reviewed MRI baseline (Dai \emph{et al.}\ 2020, \emph{Med.\ Phys.}): deeply supervised 3D attention-gated U-Net trained with multi-scale Dice/BCE and weighted axial total-variation (TV) continuity regularization, followed by slice-to-slice centroid linking and cubic polynomial smoothing.
\item[d] Peer-reviewed MRI baseline (Kostoulas \emph{et al.}\ 2025, \emph{Proc.\ SPIE Med.\ Imaging}): native 3D ResUNet with multi-peak blob splitting, iterative collinear gap-bridging across discontinuous MRI slices ($\le 18$\,mm gap), false-fragment pruning, and 3rd-order polynomial fitting.
\item[e] Peer-reviewed MRI ensemble baseline (Aleong \emph{et al.}\ 2024, \emph{Med.\ Phys.}\ / Kim \emph{et al.}\ 2026, \emph{Brachytherapy}): 5-model inner-fold 3D U-Net ensemble computing voxel-wise epistemic uncertainty $\sigma_p$, disagreement-based false-track filtering, and inverse-variance-weighted 3rd-order polynomial fitting.
\item[f] Recent preprint baseline (Xiao \emph{et al.}\ 2026, \emph{arXiv:2607.11290}): implant-region gate, local PCA/Hough direction initialization, synchronous cost-matrix inward tracking, and 3rd-order polynomial fitting on OOF MRI masks.
\item[g] Clean nested \NF{} pipeline (\texttt{4a}) trained on clinical folds without \texttt{SynthBrachy3T} synthetic bundle pre-training.
\item[h] Full clean nested \NF{} pipeline (\texttt{4b}) with Stage 2B pre-trained on \texttt{SynthBrachy3T} and Stage 2C 91-step ($0.5$\,mm) 22-channel 1D Axial Tip Localizer, plus strictly nested $5\times 5$ inner-OOF Stage 1 cross-fitting (\texttt{4c}) (whole-cohort F1: $p = 1.1\times10^{-5}$ vs.\ PMB 2019, $p = 5.0\times10^{-10}$ vs.\ Aleong/Kim, $p = 1.8\times10^{-10}$ vs.\ Kostoulas 2025, $p = 3.8\times10^{-9}$ vs.\ Dai 2020, $p = 3.0\times10^{-10}$ vs.\ Xiao 2026, $p = 5.2\times10^{-10}$ vs.\ clDice; \Etip{}: $p \le 6.9\times10^{-7}$ vs.\ all six external methods).
\item[i] \NF{} (\texttt{4b}) augmented with three complementary mechanisms reused from the peer-reviewed MRI literature (\texttt{4d}): (A) Kim \emph{et al.}\ (2026) inverse-variance and sub-voxel dark-core weighted Poly3 shaft fitting ($E_{\text{shaft}}$ reduced from $0.589$ to $0.584$\,mm, $p = 1.8\times 10^{-8}$), (B) Dai \emph{et al.}\ (2020) directional axial TV continuity + Aleong \emph{et al.}\ (2024) 5-model epistemic uncertainty gating and 24-channel 1D tip profiling (eliminating 6 false tracks with 0 lost catheters, F1 $89.1\,\% \to 89.4\,\%$, $p = 0.028$; axial median tip error $1.99 \to 1.94$\,mm), and (C) Kostoulas \emph{et al.}\ (2025) iterative multi-segment gap-bridging (reaching $92.0\,\%$ recall, $90.5\,\%$ long $F1_{\text{adj}}$, $91.8\,\%$ adhesion F1, $0.579$\,mm mean / $0.463$\,mm median shaft error, and $6.21$\,mm mean tip error when combined with inner-OOF cross-fitting).
\item[j] Evaluated on the 759 full-depth \texttt{CY} interstitial shafts; predictions matching the 304 auxiliary \texttt{HT} short obturator tracks are not penalized as false positives ($94.7\,\%$ recall at $\tau=35\,\%$).
\item[k] The 23/54 patients with $\ge 4$ long or $\ge 6$ total catheters passing within $\le 3.0$\,mm of a neighbour (571 catheters evaluated, 191 adhering; paired 23-patient Wilcoxon for \texttt{4b}/\texttt{4d}: F1 $p = 0.0012$ vs.\ PMB 2019 and $p \le 4.8\times 10^{-7}$ vs.\ the other five methods; \Etip{} $p \le 4.5\times 10^{-6}$ vs.\ all six methods).
\item[l] Oracle upper-bound check following Xiao \emph{et al.}\ (2026b) Table~2 Part~B (tracker run directly on ground-truth binary masks). Source: \texttt{2026-10-23-unified-clean-nested-benchmark.json} and \texttt{2026-10-24-peer-reviewed-baselines-and-fusion.json}.
