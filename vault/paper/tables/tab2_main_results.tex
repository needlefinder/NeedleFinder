\begin{tablenotes}[flushleft]\footnotesize
\item Rec./Prec./F1: strict one-to-one Hungarian matching ($\le 3.5$\,mm shaft threshold); \Eshaft: in-plane centerline distance on matched slices; \Etip: 3D distal-tip distance (P95 on whole cohort: $27.27$\,mm for \NF{} + Synth + 1DTip vs.\ $52.70$\,mm for PMB 2019, $45.76$\,mm for clDice, $62.69$\,mm for Xiao 2026).
\item[a] Adaptation of Zaffino \emph{et al.}\ (2019): $1\times1\times1$\,mm$^3$ isotropic 3D U-Net, retrained on our 5-fold splits, followed by slice-peak extraction, collinear chaining, and polynomial fitting.
\item[b] Native-resolution 3D ResUNet trained with Dice + focal + soft-skeleton clDice loss (2021), followed by distance-transform ridge extraction and inter-slice chaining.
\item[c] Adaptation of Xiao \emph{et al.}\ (2026): geometric implant-region gate, connected-component and distance-transform candidates with local PCA direction initialisation, synchronous cost-matrix inward tracking, and 3rd-order polynomial fitting on OOF masks.
\item[d] Clean nested \NF{} pipeline (\texttt{4a}) trained on clinical folds without \texttt{SynthBrachy3T} synthetic bundle pre-training.
\item[e] Full clean nested \NF{} pipeline (\texttt{4b}) with Stage 2B pre-trained on \texttt{SynthBrachy3T} and Stage 2C Dedicated 91-step ($0.5$\,mm) 22-channel 1D Axial Tip Localizer, plus strictly nested $5\times 5$ inner-OOF Stage 1 cross-fitting (\texttt{4c}) (whole-cohort F1: $p = 1.1\times10^{-5}$ vs.\ PMB 2019, $p = 5.2\times10^{-10}$ vs.\ clDice, $p = 3.0\times10^{-10}$ vs.\ Xiao 2026, $p = 0.32$ vs.\ \texttt{4a}, $p = 0.80$ vs.\ \texttt{4c}; \Etip{}: $p = 1.7\times10^{-8}$ vs.\ PMB 2019, $p = 6.2\times10^{-10}$ vs.\ Xiao 2026).
\item[f] Evaluated on the 759 full-depth \texttt{CY} interstitial shafts; predictions matching the 304 auxiliary \texttt{HT} short obturator tracks are not penalized as false positives ($94.7\,\%$ recall at $\tau=35\,\%$).
\item[g] The 23/54 patients with $\ge 4$ long or $\ge 6$ total catheters passing within $\le 3.0$\,mm of a neighbour (571 catheters evaluated, 191 adhering; paired 23-patient Wilcoxon for \texttt{4b}: F1 $p = 0.0012$ vs.\ PMB 2019 and $p = 4.8\times 10^{-7}$ vs.\ clDice and Xiao 2026; \Etip{} $p = 4.5\times 10^{-6}$ vs.\ PMB 2019, $p = 7.2\times 10^{-7}$ vs.\ clDice, and $p = 2.4\times 10^{-7}$ vs.\ Xiao 2026).
\item[h] Oracle upper-bound check following Xiao \emph{et al.}\ (2026b) Table~2 Part~B (tracker run directly on ground-truth binary masks). Source: \texttt{2026-10-23-unified-clean-nested-benchmark.json}.
