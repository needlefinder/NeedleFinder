\item[d] Reference-free Grubbs' (1948) three-cornered-hat intrinsic source error SD across the $n=432$ three-way matched catheters (formed by intersecting the $476$ all-catheter human pairs with OOF \NF{} predictions within $\le 3.5$\,mm).
