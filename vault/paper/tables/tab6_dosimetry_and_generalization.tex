\begin{tablenotes}[flushleft]\footnotesize
\item[a] TG-43U1 evaluation uses $5.0$\,mm dwell step spacing ($3.5$\,mm distal tip offset, $\le 45$\,mm active length) on a $1.5$\,mm isotropic 3D dose grid with implant-conformal $\text{HR-CTV}_{\text{implant}}$ ($8.0$\,mm radial envelope, $D_{90}=7.0$\,Gy) and $2\,\text{cc}$ OAR proxy shells. $|\Delta D_{90}|$ cells report mean / median percentage target dose shift; $\gamma_{2/2}$ and $\gamma_{3/3}$ are global 3D gamma pass rates ($2\,\%/2$\,mm and $3\,\%/3$\,mm). Dwell-weighted tip error $E_{\mathrm{tip}}^{\mathrm{dwell\text{-}w}}$ correlates with $|\Delta D_{90}|$ at Spearman $\rho=0.8629$ ($p=4.9\times 10^{-17}$) and with $\gamma_{2\%/2\mathrm{mm}}$ at $\rho=-0.8474$ ($p=6.5\times 10^{-16}$).
\item[b] Paired Wilcoxon vs.\ PMB 2019 ($p=4.9\times 10^{-6}$ on $|\Delta D_{90}|$, $p=8.2\times 10^{-7}$ on $\gamma_{2\%/2\mathrm{mm}}$), vs.\ Stage 0 ($p=7.9\times 10^{-8}$ on $|\Delta D_{90}|$, $p=1.4\times 10^{-7}$ on $\gamma_{2\%/2\mathrm{mm}}$), and vs.\ Human Rater 2 on $n=40$ ($p=0.0278$ on $|\Delta D_{90}|$, $p=0.0109$ on $\gamma_{2\%/2\mathrm{mm}}$).
\item[c] Part~B1 simulates a retrospective 1-click distal tip relocation oracle ($E_{\mathrm{tip}} \to \sqrt{\Delta r_\perp^2 + \min(|\Delta s_\parallel|, 0.85\,\text{mm})^2}$ when $E_{\mathrm{tip}}>2.0$\,mm, $E_{\mathrm{shaft}}$ unchanged; `Reloc.` = total tips relocated across 54 patients) ranked by out-of-fold $\hat{\sigma}_{\mathrm{tip}}$. Part~B2 reports unreviewed ($k=0$) 5-fold split-conformal 3D tip prediction interval half-widths (radii $r$ in mm) across the $n=972$ matched OOF catheters (`Fixed`: unscaled residual quantile; `Low-`$\hat{\sigma}$ $r$: median half-width on the lower-uncertainty half of catheters; `Width red.`: adaptive vs.\ fixed median half-width reduction). Simulated oracle count-guided recovery ($0.65$ clicks/patient) recovers $+17$ missed catheters ($972\to 989/1\,063$, $93.0\,\%$ recall).
\item[d] In Part~C, `Zero-Exp. No-SSL` denotes the 5-fold GYN ensemble trained with zero prostate exposure (excluding Case~100 from SSL pre-training). In 4D monitoring (4 patients, Cases 68--71, 23 serial scans, $1.31$\,s mean monitoring runtime), per-case final-scan F1s (Independent 3D $\to$ Causal 4D Kalman) are Case~68: $77.2\,\%\to 83.0\,\%$, Case~69: $84.7\,\%\to 82.7\,\%$ (precision $83.7\,\%\to 93.9\,\%$), Case~70: $85.7\,\%\to 93.3\,\%$, and Case~71: $76.2\,\%\to 66.7\,\%$ (precision $72.7\,\%\to 100.0\,\%$); mean tip error improves from $11.23\to 9.75$\,mm. Persisted catheters within $\le 10$\,mm of newly inserted needles exhibit $1.44$\,mm median ($1.61$\,mm mean) lateral shift vs.\ $1.10$\,mm at $10\text{--}20$\,mm and $1.00$\,mm ($1.19$\,mm mean) at $>20$\,mm (Mann--Whitney $p=9.97\times 10^{-4}$ for $\le 12$\,mm vs.\ $>12$\,mm); depth deflection reaches $3.29$\,mm median ($4.56$\,mm mean, $10.49$\,mm P90) at $>90$\,mm depth ($\rho=0.555, p=5.55\times 10^{-9}$). In Pelvic CT, 38 of 40 scanned CT volumes pair with the 54-case MRI benchmark ($801$ reference catheters; $68.9\,\%$ matched at $\le 3.5$\,mm, $79.2\,\%$ at $\le 5.0$\,mm; $52/100$ [$52.0\,\%$] extra MRI detections vs.\ \texttt{CY} confirmed on CT).
