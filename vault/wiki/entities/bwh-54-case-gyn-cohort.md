# Brigham & Women's Hospital 54-Case GYN Cohort

## Cohort Summary
- **Institution**: Advanced Multimodality Image Guided Operating (AMIGO) Suite, Brigham and Women's Hospital, Harvard Medical School, Boston, MA.
- **Scanner**: 3T Siemens Magnetom Verio (wide-bore), T2-weighted turbo spin-echo (TSE) axial acquisitions.
- **Total Cases**: 54 clinical cases numbered between `Case 002` and `Case 078` in `/Users/guillaume/Dropbox/GYN Cases/`.
- **Total Annotated Catheters**:
  - **760 long interstitial catheters** across 54 cases (`760CYScenes.txt`).
  - **543 dual-observer catheters** across 41 cases (`543LB_AMe_scenes.txt`).
  - **66–80+ short vaginal obturator catheters** documented in `short needle segment data of 50 cases.xlsx` (bringing total catheters in the 50-case PMB 2019 study to **826 catheters**).

## Resolution Range
- **In-plane spacing ($s_x, s_y$)**: $0.234\text{ mm}$ to $0.625\text{ mm}$ (matrix $256 \times 256$ to $640 \times 576$).
- **Slice thickness ($s_z$)**: $1.5\text{ mm}$ to $3.0\text{ mm}$ ($52$ to $120$ slices).
