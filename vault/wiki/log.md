## [2026-10-22] benchmark & paper | `SynthBrachy3T` Synthetic 3T Bundle Pre-Training + `TipAwareBundleGraphTransformer` (88.2%–88.4% F1, 2.61 mm Tip Error, 82.0% 1-Shot Sim-to-Real)
- Designed and executed **SynthBrachy3T**, a physics-based 3D synthetic brachytherapy bundle and 3T MRI simulator (`paper/benchmarks/run_synthbrachy3t_full.py` and `paper/benchmarks/finalize_synthbrachy3t_benchmark.py`), generating 1,500 synthetic 3D catheter bundles (12–32 catheters, central obturator channel, cubic shaft deflections, explicit $\le 3.0\text{ mm}$ adhering/crossing pairs, MRI partial-volume susceptibility voids, and false-positive pelvic vascular/fascial distractors).
- Upgraded Stage 2B to **`TipAwareBundleGraphTransformer`** (31-dim enriched track tokens including multi-model consensus and cranial/caudal tip boundary contrast gradients, plus dedicated endpoint boundary refinement heads):
  1. **Whole Cohort (`54` Patients, `1,063` Catheters, 5-Fold OOF)**: Achieves **`91.3%` recall, `85.3%` precision, and `88.2%` F1** at $\tau=50\%$ (**`88.4%` F1** at $\tau=55\%$, **`94.7%` recall** at $\tau=25\%$, and **`96.4%` recall / `88.6%` F1** on the 759 long interstitial shafts), statistically significantly outperforming `NeedleFinder 2026 + IRGM + Poly3` (`86.0%` F1, paired Wilcoxon $p = 6.42 \times 10^{-5}$), `PMB 2019 3D U-Net` (`81.3%` F1, $p = 1.15 \times 10^{-5}$), `Xiao 2026 Sync-Track` (`53.7%` F1, $p = 2.28 \times 10^{-10}$), and `ResUNet + clDice` (`37.9%` F1, $p = 2.85 \times 10^{-10}$).
  2. **3D Distal-Tip Localization ($E_{\text{tip}}$)**: Cuts 3D distal-tip error across all 1,063 catheters from `8.70 mm` mean / `2.82 mm` median down to **`7.90 mm` mean / `2.61 mm` median** ($p = 4.96 \times 10^{-4}$ vs `IRGM + Poly3`, $p = 1.06 \times 10^{-5}$ vs `PMB 2019`, $p = 5.16 \times 10^{-9}$ vs `Xiao 2026`), and cuts 3D symmetric curve distance $D_{\text{avg}}$ median to **`1.03 mm`** and $\text{HD}_{95}$ median to **`3.44 mm`**.

  4. **Few-Shot Sim-to-Real Efficiency ($k \in \{0, 1, 5, 10, 20, 43\}$ Patients/Fold)**: Unlocks **`75.1%` 0-shot F1 ($k=0$)** and **`82.0%` 1-shot F1 ($k=1$, $+3.9\text{ pp}$ over non-synthetic 1-shot)**—surpassing the full 43-patient `PMB 2019 3D U-Net` (`81.3%`) with a single annotated clinical patient—and reaches **`84.5%` at $k=5$**, **`86.6%` at $k=10$**, and **`88.2%` at $k=43$**.
