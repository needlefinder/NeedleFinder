## Summary
EMBRACE-I was a prospective observational multicentre cohort study (24 centres in Europe, Asia and North America; NCT00920920) of chemoradiotherapy followed by MRI-based image-guided adaptive brachytherapy (IGABT) in locally advanced cervical cancer (FIGO IB-IVA, or IVB limited to para-aortic nodes). Accrual ran from 30 July 2008 to 29 Dec 2015; 1416 patients were registered, 1341 were analysed for disease outcome and 1251 for morbidity. MRI-based IGABT with dose optimisation was done in 1317 (98.2 %) of 1341 patients. Median CTV_HR volume was 28 cm3 (IQR 20-40) and median CTV_HR D90 was 90 Gy EQD2 (IQR 85-94). At a median follow-up of 51 months, actuarial 5-year local control was 92 % (95 % CI 90-93). Actuarial 5-year grade 3-5 morbidity was 6.8 % genitourinary, 8.5 % gastrointestinal, 5.7 % vaginal and 3.2 % fistulae. The authors conclude that MRI-based IGABT gives effective, stable long-term local control with limited severe morbidity and can serve as a benchmark.
