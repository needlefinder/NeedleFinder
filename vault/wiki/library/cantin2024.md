## Summary
Phantom proof-of-concept of an electromagnetic (EM) tracking guidance workflow for interstitial needle placement in gynecologic HDR brachytherapy, fusing a pre-implant 3D T2W MRI with real-time 3D TRUS. A clinical investigational Philips system (TPS plus EM tracking, originally designed for prostate) was used with a custom 5-DOF stylet and a virtual 250-hole template; one retrospectively chosen cervical cancer implant (Elekta CT/MR ring applicator plus 13 interstitial needles) was reproduced in a water phantom. EM tracking accuracy was below 1 mm in both sagittal and transverse TRUS modes (US-to-EM calibration residuals 0.6 +/- 0.2 mm sagittal, 0.8 +/- 0.2 mm transverse); MRI contours were propagated to TRUS after rigid registration and needle insertion was guided in real time. Limitations: the ring channel could only be half reconstructed and the preplan template only supports parallel needle patterns.

## Key facts for our paper
- Setting: water phantom, single GYN implant, EM tracking with MRI-TRUS fusion (abstract; Methods).
- EM tracking accuracy < 1 mm (abstract); calibration residuals 0.6 and 0.8 mm (Results).
- Requires an EM field generator, tracked stylet, TRUS stepper and dedicated TPS (Methods).
