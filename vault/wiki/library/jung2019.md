## Summary
Deep-learning assisted auto-digitization of interstitial needles for 3D CT-based interstitial HDR brachytherapy of gynecological cancer. Step 1: a U-net segments all needles from CT. Step 2: the segmented voxels are simultaneously clustered into needle groups and the central trajectories generated by solving an optimisation problem. Evaluated on ten patient cases not used in training (average 20.7 needles per case): mean Dice between automatic and manual segmentation 0.93; Hausdorff distance between automatic and physicist-digitised trajectories about 0.71 mm on average; mean tip-position difference about 0.63 mm; about 5 min per case. The authors consider the accuracy acceptable for HDR planning.

## Key facts for our paper
- Modality CT; site gynecological interstitial HDR; 10 test cases, 20.7 needles per case on average (Abstract).
- U-net segmentation + optimisation-based clustering/trajectory generation (Abstract).
