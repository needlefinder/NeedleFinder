## Summary
Roumeliotis et al. (Johns Hopkins, with Schmidt and Viswanathan) report the prospective implementation of MR-guided position-location tracking (MRT) for template-based (Syed/Neblett) interstitial gynecologic brachytherapy between 2018 and 2023, comparing 18 prospectively enrolled MRT patients with 62 standard implants. The MRT system was used without interruption from device-related events in all 18 patients; mean procedure time was 96 +/- 27 min with MRT versus 100 +/- 26 min for standard implants, supporting non-inferiority (p = 0.03). In the subgroup of primary vaginal or recurrent endometrial cancer without a uterus, CTV D90 was 75.2 Gy with MRT versus 66.4 Gy without (p = 0.02), while organ-at-risk dose differences were not significant.

## Key facts for our paper
- 18 MRT vs 62 standard Syed/Neblett interstitial implants (2018-2023); procedure time 96 vs 100 min.
- Continuation of the BWH MR-tracking lineage [@wang2015; @dearcos2017] at JHU; same group as [@kim2026].
- Documents a mean interstitial procedure time of about 100 min, of which reconstruction is a part.
