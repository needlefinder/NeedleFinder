## Summary
Rylander et al. evaluated the dosimetric impact of target contouring and needle reconstruction uncertainties in US-, CT- and MRI-based HDR prostate brachytherapy planning, using US, CT and MR images acquired after needle insertion in 22 procedures of 11 consecutive patients, with MRI-based planning as the reference. Uncertainties in US- and CT-based planning decreased the planned CTV-prostate D90 by a mean of 2.9 +/- 5.0 Gy (p = 0.03) and 2.9 +/- 2.9 Gy (p = 0.001), respectively, while intra-observer contouring variation on MRI produced a 1.6 +/- 1.5 Gy variation. Needle reconstruction uncertainty on US caused a dose variation of +/- 3 Gy to the urethra. Inter-modality uncertainties (US and CT versus MR) were larger than the MR intra-observer uncertainty, and US/CT planning systematically overestimated the prescribed target dose.

## Key facts for our paper
- 11 patients / 22 procedures; US, CT and MRI acquired post-insertion; MRI-based planning as reference.
- Reconstruction + contouring uncertainty: CTV D90 decrease of 2.9 Gy (US and CT); urethra +/- 3 Gy from US needle reconstruction uncertainty.
- Supports the claim that MRI-based planning reduces inter-modality uncertainty.
