## Summary
Shit et al. propose Relationformer, a unified one-stage transformer framework that jointly predicts objects and their relations for image-to-graph generation, with blood-vessel network extraction and road network extraction as target applications. Instead of the traditional two-stage approach (object detection followed by separate relation prediction, which prevents simultaneous object-relation interaction), the model uses direct set-based object prediction and introduces a learnable [rln]-token that, together with the [obj]-tokens, performs local and global semantic reasoning and yields computationally efficient pairwise relation prediction. The authors report state-of-the-art performance on multiple diverse, multi-domain datasets.

## Key facts for our paper
- One-stage transformer that outputs a graph (nodes + edges) directly from an image; vessel-network extraction is a target domain.
- Same group as clDice [@shit2021]; relevant to our Stage 2B bundle graph transformer that reasons over all trajectories jointly.
