## Summary
Jacobsen et al. present joint consensus imaging recommendations of the Society of Abdominal Radiology and the American Brachytherapy Society for image-guided cervical cancer brachytherapy, covering evaluation and procedural guidance. Image guidance during applicator implantation reduces the rate of uterine perforation by the tandem; post-implant images may be acquired with radiography, CT or MRI, with CT or MRI preferred because of fewer severe complications. Pre-brachytherapy T2-weighted MRI may serve as a reference for HR-CTV contouring when CT is used for planning, and reference CT and MRI protocols are provided.

## Key facts for our paper
- SAR/ABS 2022 consensus: CT or MRI preferred for post-implant imaging; reference MRI protocols provided.
- Establishes MRI (T2W) as the reference modality for target delineation.
