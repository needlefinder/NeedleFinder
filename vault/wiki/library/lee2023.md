## Summary
Lee et al. investigated the feasibility of MRI-only treatment planning (MRTP) for interstitial HDR brachytherapy in gynecologic cancer patients using a clinically available PETRA ("pointwise encoding time reduction with radial acquisition") sequence optimized to improve interstitial catheter-tissue contrast. Interstitial needles outside the obturator region were reconstructed on MR images alone, whereas for catheters passing through the obturator a library-based reconstruction was proposed, with the clinical CT-based dwell coordinates used as a surrogate in this work; dwell times were copied from the clinical plans. Catheter reconstruction was possible on PETRA images, with an overall reconstruction difference of 1.7 ± 0.5 mm compared with the CT-based reconstruction, attributed to registration errors. CTV D90 differed by 1.5 ± 1.9% (physical) and 0.7 ± 1.0 Gy (EQD2); D2cc EQD2 differences were -0.4 ± 1.1%, 0.5 ± 2.8% and -0.5 ± 1.4% for rectum, bladder and sigmoid. The authors describe the work as a proof-of-concept study showing MRTP with PETRA is feasible with dosimetry comparable to the CT/MRI approach; the number of patients and catheters is not given in the abstract.

## Key facts for our paper
- Gynecologic interstitial HDR; PETRA sequence; MRI-only reconstruction of needles outside the obturator; CT-derived dwell coordinates used as surrogate library for obturator catheters (abstract).
- Reconstruction difference vs CT 1.7 ± 0.5 mm (abstract).
- CTV D90 difference 1.5 ± 1.9% / 0.7 ± 1.0 Gy EQD2; OAR D2cc differences within about ±0.5% (abstract).
- Self-described "proof-of-concept study" (abstract, Significance).

## How our manuscript cites it
- "MRI-only interstitial workflows have been validated clinically [@shaaer2020; @lee2023]": NOT SUPPORTED for lee2023 as worded. The source calls itself a proof-of-concept feasibility study, and obturator catheters were taken from the CT reconstruction rather than from MRI. Suggested wording: "MRI-only interstitial workflows have been shown to be feasible [@shaaer2020; @lee2023]".
- "zero-echo-time sequences that show the catheter lumen [@lee2023]": NOT CHECKABLE (abstract only). The abstract describes PETRA only as "a clinically available MRI sequence optimized to improve interstitial catheter-tissue contrast"; it does not say "zero-echo-time" nor mention the catheter lumen. If the full text does not support "lumen", use "ultrashort/zero-echo-time sequences such as PETRA that improve catheter-tissue contrast [@lee2023]".
