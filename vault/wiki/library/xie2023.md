## Summary
Evaluates deep-learning auto-reconstruction of metallic interstitial needles on 3D CT for post-operative cervical cancer brachytherapy. 70 patients (Aug 2021 to Jul 2022), each treated with three metallic needles, CT at 3 mm slice thickness; the 70 patients were split 50:10:10 into training, validation and test. The model is an nnU-Net (3D U-Net configuration) with histogram equalization preprocessing. Geometric accuracy per needle: mean DSC 0.88 +/- 0.03, 0.89 +/- 0.02 and 0.90 +/- 0.02; 95% Hausdorff distance 0.77 +/- 0.12, 0.73 +/- 0.13 and 0.71 +/- 0.07 mm; Jaccard 0.81, 0.80, 0.81. No significant dosimetric differences between manual and automatic reconstruction (Wilcoxon, p > 0.05); Spearman correlation between geometric metrics and dose differences was weak. Stated limitations: single applicator type (not vaginal or Fletcher applicators) and single-centre training data.

## Key facts for our paper
- Modality/site: CT, post-operative cervical cancer, three metallic needles per patient (Methods, full text).
- Cohort: 70 patients; split 50/10/10 train/validation/test (Methods, "Design of the nnU-Net"). 70 x 3 = 210 needles overall; test set is 10 patients (30 needles).
- Method: nnU-Net, 3D U-Net configuration (Methods).
- DSC 0.88 / 0.89 / 0.90; HD95 0.77 / 0.73 / 0.71 mm; JC 0.81 / 0.80 / 0.81 for the three needles (Results, Fig. 3).
- No shaft/tip centreline error or recall/precision is reported; no run time is reported.
