## Summary
Two-stage, data-efficient pipeline for automatic multi-catheter digitization in CT-guided interstitial brachytherapy, addressing annotation scarcity and catheter adhesion. Data: 203 post-implant CT fractions from 38 patients treated for locally recurrent cervical cancer at Peking University Third Hospital (Nov 2017 to Apr 2023), 4-13 catheters per fraction (mean 7.31), 2-5 mm slices resampled to 5 mm; catheter adhesion present in 83/203 fractions. Stage 1: a 2D implant region-aware network (IRNet) with an implant region gating module (IRGM: an implant-region estimator plus adaptive gating blocks) is pretrained on synthetic CT volumes with simulated metallic catheters and fine-tuned with only 10 clinical fractions. Stage 2: a structure-aware reconstruction module applies an iterative, direction-constrained 3D Hough transform (after Dalitz et al.) with CT-intensity-weighted PCA refinement on the external catheter segments, then synchronous physics-constrained inward tracking with cost-based direction assignment, and third-order polynomial curve fitting. Patient-level five-fold cross-validation: 10-shot IRNet HD95 0.853 +/- 0.362 mm; end-to-end F1 0.891 +/- 0.178, shaft error 0.334 +/- 0.367 mm, tip error 0.896 +/- 0.680 mm (fully supervised IRNet: F1 0.909, recall 0.911, precision 0.907, shaft 0.311, tip 0.878); adhesion subgroup F1 0.843 +/- 0.190; about 11.6 s per case. Table 2 Part B repeats the comparison with ground-truth masks as input (proposed: shaft 0.086 mm, tip 0.099 mm, F1 0.921).

## Key facts for our paper
- 38 patients, 203 fractions, CT, locally recurrent cervical cancer, Peking University Third Hospital (Sect. 2.1).
- 4-13 catheters per fraction, mean 7.31; adhesion in 83/203 fractions (Sect. 2.1, 2.4.1).
- Whole cohort, end-to-end, 10-shot fine-tuned IRNet: recall 0.894, precision 0.890, F1 0.891 +/- 0.178, E_shaft 0.334 +/- 0.367 mm, E_tip 0.896 +/- 0.680 mm (Table 2 Part A; Abstract).
- Whole cohort, end-to-end, fully supervised IRNet: recall 0.911 +/- 0.177, precision 0.907 +/- 0.182, F1 0.909 +/- 0.179, E_shaft 0.311 +/- 0.269 mm, E_tip 0.878 +/- 0.585 mm (Table 2 Part A).
- Adhesion subgroup end-to-end: F1 0.844 (fully supervised) / 0.843 +/- 0.190 (10-shot) (Table 2 Part A).
- Ground-truth-mask input (Table 2 Part B), proposed tracker: E_shaft 0.086 +/- 0.207 mm, E_tip 0.099 +/- 0.300 mm, F1 0.921; adhesion subgroup F1 0.875.
- Matching rule for a true positive: shaft matching cost <= 3.0 mm and a tip criterion (Sect. 2.4.3).
- Runtime: approximately 11.6 s per case (Abstract; Results).
- Components: IRGM (implant region estimator + adaptive gating), direction-constrained iterative 3D Hough transform based on Dalitz et al., CT-intensity-weighted PCA, synchronous physics-constrained inward tracking with cost-based direction assignment, third-order polynomial regularisation (Sect. 2.2.2, 2.3.2).
