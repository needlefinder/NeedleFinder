## Summary
Protocol/position paper describing the EMBRACE II study, an interventional and observational multicentre study in locally advanced cervical cancer that builds on the GEC-ESTRO recommendations, RetroEMBRACE and EMBRACE I. It aims to benchmark high local, nodal and systemic control with limited morbidity, using an advanced target selection and contouring protocol for EBRT and brachytherapy, a multi-parametric brachytherapy dose prescription protocol with soft planning aims and hard dose limits, and advanced EBRT (IMRT/IGRT) and brachytherapy (IC/IS) techniques. Table 4 of the paper lists the EMBRACE II planning aims: CTV_HR D90 > 90 Gy and < 95 Gy EQD2 (limit for prescribed dose > 85 Gy), GTV_res D98 > 95 Gy, CTV_IR D98 > 60 Gy, point A > 65 Gy; OAR aims bladder D2cm3 < 80 Gy, rectum D2cm3 < 65 Gy, recto-vaginal point < 65 Gy, sigmoid and bowel D2cm3 < 70 Gy (limits 90/75/75/75/75 Gy). Preliminary EMBRACE I findings cited suggest limiting bladder D2cm3 to <= 80 Gy.

## Key facts for our paper
- EMBRACE II planning aim CTV_HR D90: > 90 Gy and < 95 Gy EQD2(alpha/beta = 10); hard limit > 85 Gy (Table 4).
- OAR planning aims (EQD2, alpha/beta = 3): bladder D2cm3 < 80 Gy, rectum D2cm3 < 65 Gy, recto-vaginal point < 65 Gy, sigmoid D2cm3 < 70 Gy, bowel D2cm3 < 70 Gy; limits for prescribed dose 90 / 75 / 75 / 75 / 75 Gy (Table 4).
- Study is restricted to locally advanced cervical cancer (Abstract; Sect. on study design).
- Multicentre; EBRT 45 Gy/25 fractions is included in the EQD2 totals (Table 4 caption).

## How our manuscript cites it
- "For locally advanced cervical, vaginal, and recurrent endometrial cancers it is the cornerstone of curative radiotherapy [@potter2018; @potter2021]" (Intro). SUPPORTED for locally advanced cervical cancer. NOT SUPPORTED for vaginal and recurrent endometrial cancers: EMBRACE II (and EMBRACE-I) concern cervical cancer only. Suggested fix: cite a vaginal/endometrial brachytherapy source for those sites, or restrict the sentence to "locally advanced cervical cancer".
- "EMBRACE-II now prescribes planning aims of 90 to 95 Gy while sparing the bladder, rectum, and sigmoid colon [@potter2018]" (Intro). SUPPORTED: Table 4 gives CTV_HR D90 planning aim > 90 Gy and < 95 Gy with bladder, rectum, sigmoid (and bowel, recto-vaginal point) D2cm3 aims.
- Not in Table 5.
