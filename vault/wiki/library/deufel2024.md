## Summary
Describes EMVision, an electromagnetic tracking (EMT) guidance system for image-guided placement of interstitial brachytherapy needles. It registers CT and EMT frames with an automated point-based algorithm using tandem/ovoid locations, then guides needle placement and digitization with an intraluminal EMT sensor and a user interface showing the needle in axial, coronal, sagittal and 3D CT views, with contour and pre-planned needle overlays and 6-DOF compensation for tandem motion. Accuracy was evaluated against post-implant CT in a phantom and a human cadaver: in phantom 0.76 +/- 0.13 mm for needle tips (up to 75 mm from the tandem/ovoids) and 0.52 +/- 0.27 mm for shafts (up to 100 mm); in cadaver 0.77 +/- 0.14 mm (tip) and 0.40 +/- 0.21 mm (shaft).

## Key facts for our paper
