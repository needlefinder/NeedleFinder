## Summary
Hu et al. propose a continuous-valued, differentiable topological loss that enforces a segmentation to have the same topology as the ground truth, that is, the same Betti number, and incorporate it into end-to-end training of a deep network. Segmentation algorithms are prone to topological errors such as broken connections on fine-scale structures; the method achieves much better Betti number error, which directly accounts for topological correctness, and superior performance on other topology-relevant metrics such as the Adjusted Rand Index and Variation of Information, on a broad spectrum of natural and biomedical datasets.

## Key facts for our paper
- First widely used differentiable topology (Betti-number) loss for segmentation; targets broken connections in fine structures.
- Foundational reference for the topology-aware loss family (clDice, skeleton recall).
