## Summary
Karius et al. investigated geometric and dosimetric inter-observer variability of needle reconstruction for temporary (HDR) prostate brachytherapy on transrectal ultrasound, and the potential of CBCT-to-TRUS registration to support reconstruction. Needles implanted in 28 patients were reconstructed on TRUS by three physicists; geometric deviations and the associated dose variations to prostate, urethra, bladder and rectum were analyzed. Geometric inter-observer deviations >1 mm and >3 mm were found for 34.9% and 3.5% of all needles, respectively; prostate coverage changed by up to 7.2% and urethra dose partly exceeded constraints. In a phantom study, marker-based and probe-based CBCT-TRUS registrations gave mean needle transfer accuracies of 0.73 mm and 0.12 mm; in three patient cases the marker-based approach was superior. The abstract does not report a mean or median inter-observer deviation.
