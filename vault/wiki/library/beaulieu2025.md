## Summary
Joint AAPM/ESTRO Task Group 317 report reviewing tracking technologies that work independently of imaging systems for brachytherapy: infrared, electromagnetic, fiber-optic shape sensing (fiber Bragg grating) and active radiofrequency coil tracking. It covers their use for automated implant reconstruction, QC tasks such as channel path and tip reconstruction, and real-time guidance; reports performance and accuracy in controlled and clinically relevant environments; summarizes target accuracy and performance needed for critical tasks; discusses risk-based analysis for an electromagnetic tracking system used in a clinical trial; and lays out the elements of a quality management program and recommendations for commissioning, QA and clinical use.

## Key facts for our paper
- Scope: tracking technologies (IR, EM, FBG, active RF coil) for catheter/needle/applicator insertion guidance, reconstruction and transfer-tube connection (abstract).
- Provides guidelines on target accuracy for critical tasks and a quality management program (abstract); specific numeric tolerances are not in the abstract.
