## Summary
Introduces centerlineDice (clDice), a similarity measure computed on the intersection of segmentation masks and their morphological skeleta, for tubular network-like structures (vessels, neurons, roads) where connectedness is the key property. The authors prove that clDice guarantees topology preservation up to homotopy equivalence for binary 2D and 3D segmentation, and propose a differentiable loss (soft-clDice) for training arbitrary segmentation networks. Benchmarked on five public datasets (vessels, roads, neurons; 2D and 3D), training with soft-clDice yields segmentations with more accurate connectivity, higher graph similarity and better volumetric scores.

## Key facts for our paper
- clDice = Dice-like measure between masks and skeleta; soft-clDice is the differentiable training loss (Abstract).
- Topology preservation up to homotopy equivalence proven for binary 2D/3D (Abstract).
- Five public datasets; improved connectivity and graph similarity (Abstract).
