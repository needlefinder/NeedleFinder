## Summary
nnU-Net is a deep-learning segmentation method that configures itself automatically, including preprocessing, network architecture, training and post-processing, for any new biomedical segmentation task. The design choices are modelled as fixed parameters, interdependent rules and empirical decisions. Without manual intervention, nnU-Net surpasses most existing approaches, including highly specialised solutions, on 23 public datasets used in international biomedical segmentation competitions. It is released as an out-of-the-box tool requiring neither expert knowledge nor computing resources beyond standard network training.
