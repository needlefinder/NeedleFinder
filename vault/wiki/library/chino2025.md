## Summary
This ABS consensus statement summarizes considerations for adding interstitial needles to intracavitary implants in cervical cancer brachytherapy (hybrid IC/IS brachytherapy). A panel of gynecologic brachytherapy physicians and physicists completed surveys and met virtually to define current practice; the document was reviewed and approved by the panel and the ABS Board of Directors. The guidance covers resources for emergencies, planning considerations, applicator selection, pre-, intra- and post-procedural imaging, and approaches to challenging cases that benefit from a hybrid approach. It concludes that hybrid brachytherapy offers dosimetric advantages but increases procedural complexity. The abstract contains no numbers on needle counts, needle material, or insertion technique.
