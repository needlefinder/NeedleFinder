## Summary
Develops a U-Net model for automated catheter localization on ultrasound for HDR prostate brachytherapy and studies the factors that limit its performance. Data: 242 patients treated 2016-2020, 31,000 transverse images reconstructed from 3D sagittal US acquisitions, and 3500 implanted catheters manually localized by the planner, each ranked by severity of imaging artifacts; five-fold cross-validation over the whole dataset. Post-processing combines slice predictions with implant information to reconstruct catheters in 3D matched to grid positions, with a reconstruction confidence metric; prediction plus reconstruction took under 2 min per patient. Overall 80% of catheter reconstructions were accurate (within 2 mm along 90% of their length); tips were often not detected and had to be extrapolated. Accuracy was 89% for the easiest artifact ranking and fell to 13% for the hardest, and decreased at distal grid positions (down to 50%) even for easy catheters. The confidence metric raised accuracy to 91% while excluding 27% of reconstructions.
