## Summary
Deep-learning multi-catheter detection on MRI for HDR prostate brachytherapy. A deeply supervised attention-gated U-Net with a weighted total-variation (TV) regularization term (to encode catheter continuity along the shaft direction) was trained on binary catheter annotations from physicists. Data: 20 prostate cancer patients, T2-weighted MRI at 1.5 T (Siemens Aera) acquired after TRUS-guided catheter insertion; leave-one-out cross-validation. The method detected 299 catheters with tip error 0.37 +/- 1.68 mm and shaft error 0.93 +/- 0.50 mm; 87% of tips within +/- 2.0 mm and more than 71% within 1.0 mm; 97% of catheter shafts within 2.0 mm and 63% within 1.0 mm. Stated limitation: scans with obvious artifacts (intensity non-uniformity, motion) were excluded.

## Key facts for our paper
- Modality/site: 1.5 T T2W MRI, HDR prostate (Methods, full text).
- Cohort: 20 patients, 299 catheters; leave-one-out CV (abstract; Methods).
- Method: deeply supervised attention U-Net + TV regularization (not nnU-Net) (abstract).
- Shaft error 0.93 +/- 0.50 mm (mean); tip error 0.37 +/- 1.68 mm (mean) (abstract).
- 97% of shafts < 2.0 mm, 63% < 1.0 mm; 87% of tips within +/- 2.0 mm, > 71% within 1.0 mm (abstract).
- Manual digitization takes about 10-20 min per case per the Discussion.
