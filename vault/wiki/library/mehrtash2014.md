## Summary
Mehrtash et al. (with Pernelle, Viswanathan, Cormack and Kapur) built a benchtop study closely mimicking interstitial gynecologic brachytherapy to evaluate the accuracy of electromagnetically navigated catheter placement, using an Aurora EM tracking system with a 1.3 mm 6-DOF sensor, 6F (2 mm) brachytherapy catheters inserted through a Syed-Neblett applicator, and software built on 3D Slicer and PLUS. In two independent phantom experiments the mean targeting error was below 2.9 mm, which the authors describe as comparable to commercial clinical navigation systems. They note that navigation technology was well developed for prostate and brain biopsy and cardiac ablation but notably lacking for gynecologic HDR brachytherapy.

## Key facts for our paper
- BWH/SPL SPIE 2014 workshop-level paper: EM-navigated catheter placement for Syed-Neblett implants; mean targeting error < 2.9 mm in phantom.
- Same authors and open-source stack (3D Slicer, PLUS) as the later segmentation work.
