## Summary
Attention-gated 3D U-Net for automatic digitization of freehand interstitial needles on CT in HDR brachytherapy for cervical cancer. Seventeen patients with 56 CT-based interstitial HDR plans (4 to 6 stainless-steel trocar needles per fraction); 50 plans for training, 6 for validation. Spatial and channel attention gates were added to a 3D CNN (CNN + AG) and compared with a plain 3D CNN and with the clinically approved manual digitization. CNN + AG reached DSC about 94% (93.7%) versus 89% for CNN; needle tip and shaft errors of 1.1 mm and 1.8 mm (versus 2.0 and 3.3 mm for CNN); HR-CTV D90 dose difference 0.4% versus 1.7%. Inference took 1 to 2 s per case; training 12.6 h. Limitations: accuracy bounded by CT slice thickness; CT only, no MRI.

## Key facts for our paper
- Modality/site: CT, cervical cancer, freehand interstitial needles (abstract).
- Cohort: 17 patients, 56 plans (50 train / 6 validation) (abstract; Methods).
- Method: 3D U-Net with attention gates (not nnU-Net) (abstract).
- Tip error 1.1 mm, shaft error 1.8 mm for CNN + AG on the 6 validation plans (abstract, Results).
- Digitization time 1 to 2 s per case (Results, full text).
