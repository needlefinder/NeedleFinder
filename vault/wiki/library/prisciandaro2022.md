## Summary
AAPM Task Group 303 report, endorsed by the American Brachytherapy Society, on MRI implementation in HDR brachytherapy from simulation to treatment. The TG was charged with developing recommendations for commissioning, clinical implementation and ongoing QA, and with describing HDR workflows and practical considerations when implementing MR imaging; the report focuses on gynecologic and prostate cancer. It reviews available applicators, clinical trials and earlier TG reports, describes MRI protocols and methodologies with recommendations for clinical implementation, and gives an example of risk-based QA for MRI-based prostate HDR. An erratum was published in Med Phys 2023;50(2):1258 (doi 10.1002/mp.15991).
