## Summary
Vijayan et al. systematically reviewed (PRISMA) peer-reviewed journal articles on AI in brachytherapy published between 1 January 1980 and 1 August 2025 (PubMed, Google Scholar, Cochrane Library and institutional databases). From an initial 10,820 results, 232 studies met the inclusion criteria: 38 on applicator/needle reconstruction, 74 segmentation, 8 imaging, 40 dose calculation, 27 outcome prediction and 15 other planning applications; by site, 56 prostate, 66 gynecological, 2 breast, 3 choroidal, 3 head and neck and 35 unspecified/multiple. The review concludes that AI can produce clinically acceptable planning data in significantly less time than current practice, but that the literature remains highly retrospective. In the discussion, the authors identify only four clinical implementations (two planning/optimization, one clinical needle reconstruction for prostate HDR, one prospective automated contouring evaluation), state that all clinical implementations were single-center and that no multi-center prospective validations were identified, and call for prospective multi-institutional end-to-end validation.
