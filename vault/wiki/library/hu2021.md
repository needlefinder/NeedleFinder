## Summary
Hu et al. investigated a U-Net for automatic segmentation and reconstruction of Fletcher applicators in planning CT for cervical cancer brachytherapy. Sixty patients with Fletcher applicators were split 50:10 into training and validation, and another 10 patients formed the test set. Segmented applicator coordinates were converted into RT structure channels, and tip and shaft errors plus dosimetric differences against manual reconstruction were evaluated. On the 10 test patients, mean DSC, HD95 and reconstruction time were 0.89, 1.66 mm and 17.12 s; mean tip error was 0.80 mm and mean shaft error below 0.50 mm. Dosimetric differences were 0.29% for HR-CTV D90 and below 2.64% for OAR D2cc even with doubled maximum shaft error. The method concerns intracavitary applicators (Fletcher), not interstitial needles.

## Key facts for our paper
- CT, Fletcher applicator, 60 train/val + 10 test patients (abstract).
- U-Net; DSC 0.89, HD95 1.66 mm, 17.12 s per case; tip error 0.80 mm, shaft error <0.50 mm (abstract).
- HR-CTV D90 difference 0.29%; OAR D2cc <2.64% (abstract).
