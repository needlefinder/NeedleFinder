## Summary
GYN GEC-ESTRO Working Group recommendations on applicator commissioning and reconstruction for 3D image-based cervix cancer brachytherapy. Because of steep dose gradients, reconstruction errors can cause major dose deviations in target and OARs, so commissioning (autoradiography and imaging, CT optimal; stored as library applicators) and reconstruction methods must minimise uncertainties. The source channel is well visualised on CT, but reconstruction is more challenging on MR: commercial MRI dummy sources are scarce, titanium applicators can produce artefacts, and the MR sequence choice is essential. Para-transverse imaging with slice thickness <= 5 mm, or 3D sequences with isotropic voxels, is recommended. Contouring and reconstruction should preferably be done in the same image series to avoid fusion uncertainties. Under well-controlled conditions, reconstruction uncertainties are generally smaller than other brachytherapy uncertainties such as contouring and organ motion.
