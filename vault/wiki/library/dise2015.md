## Summary
Dise et al. developed an automatic interstitial catheter digitization tool for adaptive HDR brachytherapy with the Syed-Neblett template on CT. A region-growing algorithm, seeded by user-selected locations in each catheter and constrained by a spline model of the catheters to prevent inter-catheter crossover or incorrect digitization through air pockets, was applied to successive-day CT scans of 10 patients (14 catheters on average). All catheters were digitized successfully; the mean difference between automatic and manual positions was 0.4 +/- 0.2 mm and no significant dosimetric differences were found between automatically and manually digitized plans. The authors describe it as the first algorithm developed for adaptive interstitial brachytherapy with a large number of catheters.

## Key facts for our paper
- Syed-Neblett interstitial catheters on CT, 10 patients, ~14 catheters per patient; seeded region growing + spline constraint; 0.4 +/- 0.2 mm agreement.
- Spline/crossover constraint anticipates the adhesion problem we address; still needs manual seeds.
