## Summary
Feasibility evaluation of an MR-only workflow for interstitial gynecologic HDR brachytherapy using an in-house MR line marker for catheter reconstruction. Ten patients with locally advanced cervical cancer were included; the clinically delivered hybrid CT/MR plan served as reference. Five users manually reconstructed the catheter paths on 3D T1- and T2-weighted MR images, and their dwell positions were superimposed on the reference plans to assess the dosimetric impact. More than 96.90 % of catheter-reconstruction variations were below 2 mm. No statistically significant differences were found between MR-only and hybrid CT/MR for GTV D98, HR-CTV D90, or any OAR (bladder, sigmoid, rectum, bowel) dose metric. Average reconstruction time was 51 +/- 10 min. The authors conclude that the MR-only workflow with the MR line marker is validated as equivalent to the hybrid CT/MR approach for target coverage and OAR limits.
