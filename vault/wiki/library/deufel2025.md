## Summary
Deufel et al. used a custom electromagnetic tracking quality-assurance system (EMQA) to verify treatment-planning-system applicator reconstruction before HDR delivery in 25 gynecological intracavitary and interstitial implants. Differences between the clinical plan and EM tracking were 0.76 +/- 0.43 mm (range 0.05 to 2.48 mm) for channel tips and 0.41 +/- 0.26 mm (range 0.03 to 2.47 mm) for shafts. Dose-metric differences for the HR-CTV, bladder, rectum, large bowel and small bowel were below 2.3% on average but reached 10.5%. Agreement was better for needles implanted within a hybrid applicator than for freehand needles, and the authors conclude that an EM-based QA system can intercept rare and serious errors.

## Key facts for our paper
- 25 gyn HDR implants; TPS-vs-EM differences: tips 0.76 +/- 0.43 mm (max 2.48), shafts 0.41 +/- 0.26 mm (max 2.47).
- Dose differences < 2.3% on average, up to 10.5%.
- Freehand needles agree less well than hybrid-applicator needles; provides a patient-specific benchmark for manual reconstruction accuracy.
