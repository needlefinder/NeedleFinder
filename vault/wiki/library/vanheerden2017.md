## Summary
van Heerden et al. quantified 3 T MRI distortions on a plastic intracavitary/interstitial applicator with plastic needles and their dosimetric impact in 11 cervical cancer patients, using a B0 field map and the clinical sequence acquired with opposing readout directions. The applicator displacement ranged between 0.2 and 0.75 mm over all patients. In a worst-case scenario (twice the actual B0-induced displacement) the difference in CTV-HR D98/D90 was at most 4.7%/4.3% and the difference in bladder/rectum D2cm3 at most 6.0%/6.3%. The authors conclude that the dosimetric impact of distortions is limited and that applicator reconstruction for planning is feasible at 3 T MRI.

## Key facts for our paper
- 3 T, plastic applicator with plastic needles, 11 patients: B0-induced displacement 0.2 to 0.75 mm.
- Worst-case dosimetric impact <= 4.7% (CTV D98) and <= 6.3% (rectum D2cc).
- Establishes that geometric distortion at 3 T is sub-millimeter for plastic interstitial applicators, so reconstruction accuracy is limited by catheter visibility rather than by distortion.
