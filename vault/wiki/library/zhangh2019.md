## Summary
Zhang et al. describe the physics preparations and clinical workflow for implementing MRI-based HDR interstitial brachytherapy (MRI-HDR-IBT) for gynecologic cancers in a high-volume center, from a physicist's standpoint. Steps include testing the IBT equipment with the MRI scanner, preparing templates and catheters, preparing MRI line markers, acquiring, importing and registering MRI images, developing treatment plans, and treatment evaluation and documentation, with checklists, a TG-100-based workflow chart, a fault-tree analysis and an error-solution table. The authors list MRI-compatible applicators, MRI line markers, an HDR afterloader and an MRI-capable treatment planning system as requirements, and state that the approaches adopted from GEC-ESTRO recommendations are feasible.

## Key facts for our paper
- Workflow guide for MRI-based interstitial gyn HDR; MRI line markers listed as a required component for catheter identification.
- TG-100 fault-tree analysis of the MRI-only workflow.
