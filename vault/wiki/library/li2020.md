## Summary
Li et al. adapted Mask R-CNN to segment the needle-induced signal void on intra-procedural MR images and derived the needle feature tip location and axis orientation from the masks. The network was trained on 250 intra-procedural images from 85 MRI-guided prostate biopsy cases plus 180 real-time images from ex vivo insertions, and tested on 208 images from 40 biopsy cases and 3 real-time datasets. On the prostate data the median tip localization error was 0.71 mm and the median axis-angle difference 1.28 degrees; in real-time ex vivo MRI the tracking ran at 75 ms per image with median tip errors of about 0.9 mm. The authors note that passive needle tracking is challenged by variations of the signal-void appearance across situations.

## Key facts for our paper
- Single-needle, 2D detection-based localization on MRI (Mask R-CNN), 85 training / 40 test prostate biopsy cases.
- Median tip error 0.71 mm in-plane, axis angle error 1.28 degrees; 75 ms per image.
- Motivates detection-style (rather than voxel-segmentation) localization of the MRI signal void; predecessor of [@zhou2024] from the same UCLA group.
