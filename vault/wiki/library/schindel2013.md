## Summary
Simulated planning study of the dosimetric impact of applicator displacement and applicator reconstruction uncertainty for 20 randomly selected HDR titanium tandem-and-ovoid (T&O) plans, each evaluated as a point A plan and as an MRI-guided conformal brachytherapy (MRIG-CBT) plan. Displacement was simulated by shifting the whole T&O set cranially/caudally after dose calculation; reconstruction uncertainty by shifting each tandem and ovoid separately along its axis before dose calculation and moving the isodoses back. Rectal D2cc was the metric most sensitive to T&O displacement. To avoid more than a 10 % change, displacements had to be within +/- 1.5 mm for both plan types (a 1.5 mm displacement gave < 10 % change, average 6 %); reconstruction uncertainties had to be within +/- 3.0 mm (+/- 1.5 mm for rectal D2cc in point A plans). The dosimetric impact of displacements was significantly larger in MRIG-CBT plans than in point A plans; +/- 3 mm displacement or +/- 4.5 mm reconstruction uncertainty could cause > 10 % change.
