## Summary
Chen and Egger developed an open-source 3D Slicer module to assist MR-guided interstitial gynecologic brachytherapy. The module processes intra-operative MRI data on time over a direct DICOM connection to the scanner, then performs a multi-stage registration of CAD models of the brachytherapy devices (template and obturator) to the patient's MR images, enabling virtual placement of interstitial needles to assist the physician during the intervention.

## Key facts for our paper
- Open-source 3D Slicer module (the iGyne lineage) for the BWH AMIGO interstitial workflow: template/obturator CAD registration and virtual needle placement.
- Direct precursor of the NeedleFinder Slicer extension.
