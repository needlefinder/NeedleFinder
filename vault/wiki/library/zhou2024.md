## Summary
Automatic coarse-to-fine pipeline for localizing a single interventional needle on intra-procedural 3D MRI during MRI-guided percutaneous interventions: a 3D Swin UNETR segments the needle feature on 3D T1-weighted GRE Dixon images, a 2D reformatted image containing the needle is generated, and a 2D Swin Transformer refines the segmentation to yield the 3D tip position and axis orientation; pre-training and augmentation compensate for the small dataset. Evaluated by cross-validation on 49 in vivo intra-procedural 3D MR images from MRI-guided needle placements in the livers of seven pigs at 3 T. End-to-end time was about 6 s per volume; median Dice 0.80 (3D Swin UNETR) and 0.93 (2D Swin Transformer); median tip error 1.48 mm (1.09 pixels) and axis error 0.98 degrees; tip errors were significantly smaller than human intra-reader variation (median 1.70 mm, p < 0.01). Limitations: small preclinical dataset, cross-validation only.

## Key facts for our paper
- Modality/site: intra-procedural 3D T1w GRE MRI at 3 T, pig liver (preclinical), single needle (Methods).
- Cohort: 49 3D MR images from 7 pigs (abstract; Methods).
- Method: 3D Swin UNETR + 2D Swin Transformer (not a brachytherapy multi-catheter method) (abstract).
- Median tip error 1.48 mm; axis error 0.98 degrees; ~6 s per volume (abstract).
