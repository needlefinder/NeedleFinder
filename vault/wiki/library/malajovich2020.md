## Summary
Malajovich et al. quantified the uncertainty of four MRI-based reconstruction workflows for titanium tandem-and-ring (T&R) applicators with aqueous gel in the buildup cap: (1) proton-density-weighted MRI with a solid library applicator, (2) applicator-only reference CT fused to MRI, (3) T2-weighted MRI reconstruction following GEC-ESTRO guidance, and (4) patient CT fused to patient MRI. The 2-sigma dwell-position uncertainty was 2.7 mm (ring and tandem) for workflow 1, 1.4 mm ring / 0.8 mm tandem for workflow 2, 0.2 mm ring / 0.8 mm tandem for workflow 3, and 1.9 mm ring / 0.4 mm tandem for workflow 4. Reconstruction uncertainties produced dose variations below 10% except for workflow 1, which produced up to 20% rectal dose differences. The authors conclude that T2W MRI plans have acceptable reconstruction uncertainty and resulting dose distributions.

## Key facts for our paper
- Four MRI-based T&R reconstruction workflows compared against CT; titanium applicator with gel-filled buildup cap.
- 2-sigma dwell-position uncertainty: 2.7 mm (library model on PD-weighted MRI); 0.2 mm ring / 0.8 mm tandem (direct T2W MRI reconstruction).
- Dose variations from reconstruction uncertainty were below 10% except for the library-model workflow (up to 20% rectum).
- Supports the premise that direct reconstruction on the T2W contouring sequence is the preferred MRI-only workflow for rigid applicators.
