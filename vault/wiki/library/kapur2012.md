## Summary
Kapur et al. describe the methodology developed at Brigham and Women's Hospital to aid applicator placement and treatment planning for 3 T MR-guided gynecologic brachytherapy, used in 18 cases from September 2011 to May 2012 in the Advanced Multimodality Image Guided Operating (AMIGO) suite. A 3D balanced steady-state free precession sequence at 3 T was identified as the best sequence for catheter identification, with a ballooning artifact at the tip, and 3D treatment planning was performed on MR images. Items under development included software for virtual needle trajectory planning using probabilistic bias correction, graph-based segmentation and image registration.

## Key facts for our paper
- Origin of the BWH AMIGO 3 T MR-guided gynecologic brachytherapy program (18 cases, 2011-2012); the cohort from which our 54 patients derive.
- Identifies 3D bSSFP as best for catheter identification (ballooning tip artifact); planning directly on MRI.
